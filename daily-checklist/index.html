Seven Deadly Sins Origin Daily and Weekly Checklist

Start with rewards that expire before your next login, complete the available daily event tasks, then spend resources on one upgrade goal. Before claiming Raid rewards, check how many of this week’s two Weekly Rewards you have already used.

Updated and sources checked: October 11, 2026. Checklist priorities are editorial recommendations.

What should I do each time I log in?

  1. Check the account and mailbox. Confirm your server and character before claiming anything. Read the expiry on each mail, collect eligible expiring items, and check the balance afterward. Record a missing delivery with the item name and claim time before contacting support.
  2. Open the active event tabs. Claim today’s check-in selection, inspect daily mission progress, and collect completed mission rewards. A completed objective and a claimed reward are separate checks.
  3. Check what ends before your next session. Write down the deadline and time zone. Move expiring event tasks ahead of permanent exploration; a maintenance deadline is a changeover boundary, not an invitation to finish during maintenance.
  4. Choose one upgrade target. Name the hero, equipped weapon, and missing material. Check inventory and available reward selections before farming or spending. Stop when that bottleneck is resolved rather than upgrading every hero at once.
  5. Check any limited reward before spending its key. For Raid, read the Weekly Reward count and selected difficulty first. Extra keys do not create extra Weekly Rewards under the Version 2.2 rules.
  6. Collect and reconcile before logging out. Reopen completed event missions, inspect any exchange currency still held, and record tomorrow’s first task. Count Star Fragments when credited to your account.

If you only have a short session, finish the mailbox, check-in, and deadline checks first. Leave a longer boss run for a session when you can complete it and verify its reward.

Which current deadlines belong on my checklist?

These windows come from the linked official notices. Check for an extension or replacement notice when planning a later session.

  • Update Celebration Check-In: until the November 18 maintenance (UTC). Claim up to 14 days of selections; claiming all 14 unlocks the remaining unchosen rewards. Complete “HP Recovery!” to qualify. Check your claimed-day count, not just whether you logged in.
  • Draw Bingo: until the October 28 maintenance (UTC). Complete daily missions, collect Bingo Tickets and Chance Coins, then inspect the current board before exchanging coins for a missing number. Remaining event currencies reset when the event ends, and rewards must be claimed within the event period.
  • Exciting Cube Unboxing: until the October 28 maintenance (UTC). Cube Keys used during the event accumulate toward missions. Check the next threshold and claim completed rewards; do not spend keys on unwanted cubes solely to make the progress bar move.
  • Timespace Season 5 compensation: October 28 at 14:59 UTC. If eligible under that notice, inspect the mailbox before the deadline. This is Timespace compensation, not a confirmed Knighthood Boss Battle payout.

Time-zone check: Check-In and Bingo daily missions specifically reset at 07:00 UTC, which is 15:00 in Shanghai (UTC+8). The Timespace mailbox deadline is October 28 at 22:59 in Shanghai. These times do not establish a reset time for every game mode. No precise hour is supplied here for a deadline described only as “until maintenance.”

What should I check once a week?

  1. Raid claims: note 0/2, 1/2, or 2/2 Weekly Rewards used, the difficulties already claimed, and the group’s next planned run. Resolve the claim order before opening another Reward Cube. Use the Raid reward checklist and worked examples.
  2. Knighthood: check the current Boss Battle attempt counter and arrange a time with members. Read the current ranking issue and historical entry rules before treating a leaderboard position as a promised reward.
  3. Shops and exchanges: inspect the displayed purchase limits and refresh timers. List only items needed for your chosen upgrade, their currency cost, and the balance that will remain; a weekly refresh does not make every item worth buying.
  4. Account progress: choose one permanent quest, exploration objective, or unclaimed first-clear reward. Check that it is still unclaimed before budgeting it as income.
  5. Next week’s plan: record the nearest event deadline, one upgrade bottleneck, and one co-op appointment. Use each mode’s own timer for its reset.

How do I plan Star Fragments and keys without double-counting?

Keep three separate lines: current credited balance, eligible rewards still unclaimed, and possible future rewards. Only the first line is available to spend now. Check-ins, event missions, mailbox deliveries, and first-clear rewards can supply resources, but each has its own conditions; first-clear income cannot be farmed again.

For a pull budget, write down your target banner, its actual ticket type, the tickets you own, and the Star Fragment reserve you intend to keep. Check that banner’s bonus and carryover rules before spending. Regular Hero Draw Tickets and Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets are separate items. Do not convert an event’s maximum reward total into assumed daily income.

For example: “Today’s check-in claimed; Bingo mission done but reward unclaimed; one Raid Weekly Reward still available; no future Knighthood compensation included.” That note tells you what to collect next without inflating your balance. Keep Cube Keys, Chaos Keys, Mystic Keys, and event currencies on separate inventory lines.

Common checklist questions

Should I finish every activity every day?

Prioritize expiry and your next upgrade. A permanent collectible can wait when an eligible mailbox reward expires before your next login.

Can I farm unlimited weekly Star Fragments by repeating Raid?

No. Weekly Reward claims are capped across key types. The temporary Abyss Boss Clear bonus is a separate reward with its own version boundary; see the Raid guide before spending another key.

Does a failed delivery mean I should repeat the activity?

First inspect the reward screen, inventory, mailbox, and relevant issue notice. Keep a record of what happened; repeating an activity does not establish eligibility or recover a missing claim.
