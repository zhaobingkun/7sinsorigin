About 7sinsorigin.com

7sinsorigin.com is an independent, unofficial fan-made website focused on The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin updates, guides, banner decisions, progression, and troubleshooting.

Our goal is to help players move from a broad question to a clear next step without presenting rumors, predictions, or editorial opinions as official facts.

How we research

We prioritize Netmarble announcements, official update notes, first-party game pages, and clearly identified official social or video sources. Time-sensitive schedules, rewards, banner rules, and maintenance status are checked against the strongest available source before publication.

How we write

Pages begin with a direct answer, then separate confirmed details from practical interpretation. Archive pages preserve the context of their original patch or event. Current hubs route readers to the newest verified information instead of rewriting history.

English and Chinese coverage

The site maintains English pages at the root and Chinese pages under /zh/. Important news, guides, and current decision pages are mirrored when practical. Translations are edited for clarity and are not intended to replace the official wording.

Corrections and updates

Live-service information changes quickly. If an official notice is revised, we update the affected current page and its discovery links. To report a factual, translation, accessibility, source, or copyright issue, send the page URL and supporting detail through the Contact page.

Independence and trademarks

7sinsorigin.com is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netmarble or the owners of The Seven Deadly Sins. Game names, characters, logos, screenshots, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Editorial coverage is produced independently.

Advertising and editorial integrity

The site may display clearly identifiable advertising. Advertising does not purchase rankings, banner recommendations, or favorable coverage. If affiliate or sponsored relationships are introduced, they will be disclosed and marked appropriately.
