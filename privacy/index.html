Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information 7sinsorigin.com processes, why it is used, and the choices available to visitors.

Effective and last updated: September 17, 2026

Site operator and scope

7sinsorigin.com is an independent fan-made information site for The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin. This policy applies to the English and Chinese pages on this domain. It does not control the privacy practices of Netmarble, Google, YouTube, social networks, or other websites linked from our pages.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may process standard request data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested URL, timestamp, referring page, and security logs. We do not ask visitors to create accounts, upload files, or submit gameplay data.

Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visits, page usage, traffic sources, device categories, and site performance. Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers and may process IP and device information. Analytics is not loaded until you choose to allow analytics cookies on this site. You can change that choice through the Privacy choices link in the footer.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense or another advertising service in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies, local storage, IP address, device identifiers, and browsing information to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads, subject to your consent and applicable law. Google and its partners may serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. Where required, advertising technology will remain disabled until a valid consent choice is obtained.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites policy and manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Cookies and privacy choices

Essential storage may be used to remember your privacy preference and support basic site security. Optional analytics or advertising storage is used only after the relevant permission is granted. Choosing Reject optional keeps optional analytics disabled. Your preference is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time through Privacy choices.

Embedded and external services

Video previews do not load a YouTube player until you choose to play a video. Once opened, the YouTube no-cookie embed and Google may receive request and device information under their own policies. External links may take you to Netmarble, Google, YouTube, X, Facebook, Reddit, or other services with separate privacy practices.

Legal bases and data rights

Depending on your location, processing may rely on consent, legitimate interests in operating and securing the site, or legal obligations. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because most site data is pseudonymous or held by service providers, we may need enough information to locate a relevant record.

Retention, sharing, and international processing

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for security, analytics, legal, or operational purposes. Hosting, analytics, and advertising providers process data on our behalf or under their own terms. These providers may process information in countries outside your own, using safeguards required by applicable law.

Children

This site provides general game information and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not send personal information through email if you are not legally able to consent. A parent or guardian may contact us about a privacy concern.

Contact and updates

For privacy requests or questions, email bingkun.zhao@email.com. We may update this policy when the site, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
