7DS Origin Beginner Guide: Plan, Spend, Troubleshoot

Solve one specific problem before committing resources. Start with a reproducible decision and verified rules, not an untested “best team” list.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

Before you spend anything

Pick one immediate task: reaching a story checkpoint, surviving an encounter, claiming an expiring reward, or deciding on a banner. Do not upgrade a whole roster merely because a guide calls it a “best team.” This site does not publish a tested current tier ranking.

For an encounter, write down what failed: survival, damage before a timer, an unavailable mechanic, or control/input. Each points to a different next action. The Investment priorities explains how to compare a targeted change against its cost.

Build a testable team plan

Start with heroes you own, note the job of each slot, and test the same encounter under the same conditions. Change one thing at a time. If you change both hero and gear, you cannot tell which change helped. Record version, stage, loadout, result and failures; do not generalize one successful attempt into a universal ranking.

Use team planning for a filled example of how to diagnose and retest an account problem. Named “best” lineups need actual evidence; role labels alone are not a character recommendation.

Protect rewards without chasing every task

The current Check-In and Bingo notices require Main Quest HP Recovery!. Check that condition in the event screen first, then identify the next unclaimed reward and deadline. The Check-In and Bingo daily missions reset at 07:00 UTC; this is not evidence that every game system shares that reset.

If the game itself is failing

Before spending materials to fix poor performance, distinguish a gameplay failure from a device or service problem. Check the latest official issue notice; write down platform, client version, exact error and when it began. Do not delete local account data or download unofficial repair tools.

What is deliberately not a current guide

Earlier cooking, fishing, farming and character-role drafts did not include enough verified instructions or test conditions. They have been withdrawn from this guide directory instead of being padded with generic advice. Undated equipment/pet screenshot tables are not current-value evidence. Dated patch notices remain in the news archive with their original context.

Have a reproducible correction? Send the page URL, game version and supporting source through Contact. Do not send account credentials.

Official facts versus editorial methods

Schedules and reward quantities come from linked Netmarble notices. The worksheets and spending examples are our editorial analysis, not official optimization advice or claims of playtesting. Read the editorial policy for these boundaries.
