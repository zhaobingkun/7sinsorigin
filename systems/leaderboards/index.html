7DS Origin Leaderboards: Rewards and Issue Scope

Timespace Season 5 compensation and the October 13 Knighthood remedy are both confirmed. First identify the mode and season. Similar ranking problems do not share the same reward or reset policy.

Editorial review: October 13, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

Season 5 compensation: check eligibility separately

The official Timespace Junction notice distinguishes these rewards. It does not publish a specific hero or exploit method.

RewardWho qualifies / when
Laurel of Glory ×20 instead of final-ranking rewardsAll Season 5 leaderboard participants; after the season ends.
Star Fragment ×1,000General apology reward in the notice.
Laurel of Glory ×20 — Sector 3Players who participated in Sector 3 during that season.
Laurel of Glory ×20 — Sector 4Players who participated in Sector 4 during that season.

The apology mailbox claim window is after October 8 maintenance through October 28, 2026, 14:59 UTC. Do not add every row into a guaranteed account total: sector participation and the post-season reward are separate conditions.

What Season 6 changes, and what it does not

From Regular Season 6, the notice says participating players receive equal rewards, with no ranking-based Titles or reward differences. The schedule remains unchanged and records/rankings remain visible. It is a reward-policy change, not a statement that Timespace Junction has been removed.

Our planning advice: do not spend scarce upgrades solely to chase a higher reward bracket under this rule. A personal time target is a different goal; decide its upgrade budget separately.

What is confirmed for the Knighthood Boss Battle?

Official article 340 schedules October 13 maintenance from 05:00 to 07:00 UTC to fix the Lancelot Anchor Shot stacking issue. Article 341 then records 08:30–09:10 UTC additional maintenance after attempts and individual cumulative-score rewards failed to reset. Affected records are removed from the current leaderboard, ranking rewards are postponed, and all participants receive the same ranking rewards when they are distributed. The notices also confirm Star Fragment ×300, Knighthood Coins ×50 for the stated participants, and an individual cumulative-score total of up to 15 coins under their conditions.

For a disputed result, save mode, score, timestamp, platform and relevant screenshots for support. A fast time alone is not proof that another player broke rules. Avoid reproducing or publishing suspected exploits. See the Knighthood guide and maintenance summary for confirmed scope.

How should I check a reward or report a discrepancy?

  1. Confirm the mode and season named by the notice.
  2. Check your participation condition and the exact UTC mailbox deadline.
  3. Record the reward name, mailbox state, score, platform, and evidence if something is missing.
  4. Read later official revisions; this site cannot verify an individual account’s delivery.
