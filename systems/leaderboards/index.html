7DS Origin Leaderboards: Rewards and Issue Scope

Timespace Season 5 compensation is confirmed; Knighthood remedies are still pending. First identify the mode and season. Similar ranking problems do not share the same reward or reset policy.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

Season 5 compensation: check eligibility separately

The official Timespace Junction notice distinguishes these rewards. It does not publish a specific hero or exploit method.

RewardWho qualifies / when
Laurel of Glory ×20 instead of final-ranking rewardsAll Season 5 leaderboard participants; after the season ends.
Star Fragment ×1,000General apology reward in the notice.
Laurel of Glory ×20 — Sector 3Players who participated in Sector 3 during that season.
Laurel of Glory ×20 — Sector 4Players who participated in Sector 4 during that season.

The apology mailbox claim window is after October 8 maintenance through October 28, 2026, 14:59 UTC. Do not add every row into a guaranteed account total: sector participation and the post-season reward are separate conditions.

What Season 6 changes, and what it does not

From Regular Season 6, the notice says participating players receive equal rewards, with no ranking-based Titles or reward differences. The schedule remains unchanged and records/rankings remain visible. It is a reward-policy change, not a statement that Timespace Junction has been removed.

Our planning advice: do not spend scarce upgrades solely to chase a higher reward bracket under this rule. A personal time target is a different goal; decide its upgrade budget separately.

The October 10 Knighthood issue is a different notice

Official article 339 reports unintended scoring methods in Knighthood Boss Battle. Netmarble is reviewing measures and promises another notice. It has not announced a reset, cancellation or compensation. None of the Timespace rewards above should be applied to Knighthood.

For a disputed result, save mode/season, score, timestamp, platform and relevant screenshots for support. A fast time alone is not proof that another player broke rules. Avoid reproducing or publishing suspected exploits. See the Knighthood guide for pending versus confirmed actions.

Claim and report checklist

  1. Confirm the mode and season before using a reward notice.
  2. Check your own sector participation, then inspect the mailbox before the exact UTC deadline.
  3. If a reward is missing, record the reward name, mailbox state and eligibility evidence. Send account identifiers only through official support, not a public comment.
  4. Recheck the official notice for a later adjustment; this guide cannot confirm delivery to your individual account.

Sources and dated history

Season 5 / Season 6 notice (324), checked against the API modification on October 8, and October 10 Knighthood notice (339). For historical context, see Season 4 patch. Earlier compensation is not evidence of compensation for a new incident.
