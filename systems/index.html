Knighthood and Timespace do not share one ranking remedy

The October 10 Knighthood notice is still reviewing measures for unintended scores. The Timespace Season 5/6 notice concerns a different system. A cancellation or compensation announced for one must not be applied to the other.

An older note saying a change is planned establishes a plan at that time. Screenshot stats without a recorded build are not evidence for today's best equipment. Use the dated archive for historical changes and the Investment priorities for account decisions.
