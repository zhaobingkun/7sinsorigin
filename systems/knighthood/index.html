Knighthood System Guide

Knighthood Boss Battle, Combat Class rankings, Boss Battle rankings, and the Knighthood Shop opened after the July 22 temporary maintenance. This page explains what to join for and which weekly resets matter.

Direct Answer

Join a Knighthood if you want access to Boss Battle attempts, Knighthood rankings, and shop rewards tied to Knighthood Coins. The system now matters for weekly account progress, not just social grouping.

Knighthood Boss Battle

Any Knighthood member can enter through NPC Haro in the East District of Liones. Each player gets up to 3 challenge attempts per week, resetting every Monday at 07:00 UTC. A party can include 1 to 5 players from the same Knighthood.

How Scoring Works

Party-play scores apply equally to all party members. Each member's ranking uses their highest score across up to 3 attempts, while the highest individual score becomes the Knighthood's representative record.

Rankings

The Knighthood Rankings button adds Combat Class and Boss Battle rankings. Combat Class rankings use registered teams, while Boss Battle rankings use the best Boss Battle records. Ranking rewards are granted for Boss Battle rankings, not every ranking type.

Knighthood Shop

Boss Battle ranking rewards can grant Knighthood Coins, which are used in the Knighthood Shop. Officially listed shop items include Cutie Pretty Wand, Juicy Tomahawk, Deathwraith Shard outfit pieces, SSR Magic Crystal Shard, Platinum Ingots, Element Spell Bead Selection Chest, Large Cube Key Bundle, and Weapon Tier Up Material Selection Bundle.
