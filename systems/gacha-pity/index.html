Hero Pick Up: 80 Pity and the 120th Draw Bonus

For the checked Lancelot and Elaine Hero Pick Up events, an SSR is guaranteed within 80 draws, a non-featured SSR makes the next SSR featured, and a separate once-per-banner bonus awards the featured hero at 120 draws.

Rules and content reviewed: October 11, 2026

What does each guarantee actually mean?

“Pity” is a player term for a guarantee after a run of draws. Here it can refer to three different things. The full official October notices specify all three; treating them as one counter produces incorrect budgets.

Three mechanics in the checked Hero Pick Up notices
TermWhat it guaranteesWhat changes or preserves it
SSR pity / SSR guarantee countAn SSR within 80 draws since the previous SSR.An SSR from ordinary draws resets the count, including an off-featured SSR. The accumulated count carries to another Hero Pick Up.
Featured guarantee / guaranteed Pick Up statusAfter a non-featured SSR, the next SSR obtained is the selected Pick Up hero.It carries to another Hero Pick Up. Check the active guarantee state after obtaining a featured hero; the notice does not describe how the bonus award interacts with this flag.
120th Draw BonusThe selected banner’s featured hero as an additional bonus upon reaching 120 draws, once per banner.An early featured hero does not reset the gauge. Progress is valid only for that banner’s period and does not carry to another Hero Pick Up.

Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets are used for these hero banners. Regular Hero Draw Tickets and Exclusive Skin Draw Tickets are different resources. Both checked events require completion of the main quest HP Recovery!

Is the featured rate 50% per draw?

No. The announcement’s 50% applies when an SSR is obtained without an active featured guarantee. It does not mean half of all draws yield the featured hero, nor that draw 80 always awards that hero. The 80-draw rule guarantees rarity; the featured guarantee decides which SSR you receive after an off-featured result.

Use the event screen’s bottom-left Rate Info panel for the full obtainable-hero list and detailed probability information. The announcements do not supply a complete base SSR probability or a soft-pity progression curve. Without those, neither an expected draw cost nor an “odds by draw 70” estimate is established here.

What happens after an early SSR or a missed pickup?

Example 1: non-featured SSR on banner draw 30. Ordinary SSR pity resets to zero. The next ordinary SSR is guaranteed featured, within at most 80 further draws. If you stay on the same banner, its bonus gauge is still 30, with 90 draws to the bonus. The nearer guaranteed route is therefore at most 80 more draws.

Example 2: non-featured SSR on banner draw 80. The next ordinary SSR is guaranteed featured within 80 more draws, while the bonus is only 40 draws away. For the next featured copy, staying on this banner gives a 40-draw upper bound. A blanket “two times 80” answer ignores the bonus.

Example 3: featured SSR on banner draw 20. The ordinary SSR count resets, but the bonus gauge remains 20. Reaching 120 on this banner still awards its one-time featured bonus, so the milestone remains 100 draws away. That is an optional extra-copy objective once your first-copy goal is complete.

How do the 80 and 120 rules work together?

Before the bonus has been awarded, the conservative additional budget for the next selected featured copy is the earlier of the ordinary SSR route and the remaining bonus route. With p draws since the last SSR and b draws toward this banner’s bonus:

  • Next SSR guaranteed featured: min(80 − p, 120 − b).
  • No active featured guarantee: min(160 − p, 120 − b).

These bounds assume you continue drawing on the selected banner before it closes. The second expression allows an off-featured SSR followed by a guaranteed featured SSR; it is not a standalone maximum for the whole banner. From zero progress with an unused bonus, the actual bound for a first featured copy is 120 draws. Earlier SSRs can lower the cost, but an SSR can also reset the ordinary counter.

After the bonus has been claimed, there is no second bonus at 240. Re-read the ordinary SSR count and featured-guarantee flag to plan another copy. The notice calls the 120 reward a bonus but does not state whether its receipt resets ordinary pity or consumes the featured guarantee. This guide does not assign either behavior.

Does pity transfer when changing banners?

The checked notices explicitly preserve the accumulated Hero Pick Up draw count and guaranteed Pick Up status across another Hero Pick Up. They explicitly exclude the 120th Draw Bonus from that transfer. Read the carryover guide for switching examples and a before/after checklist. These statements do not establish transfer to Regular Hero, Selective Pick Up, or Exclusive Skin draws.

Where do these rules come from?

The full Lancelot Pick Up & Exclusive Skin notice (327) and Elaine Pick Up notice (328) were checked separately on October 11, 2026. Their hero-draw guarantee, reset, carryover, and bonus wording agrees. Elaine’s updated body names the event Whispers of the Wind, while the article header still uses its earlier name.

The Lancelot notice also covers a separate skin draw: its 10-draw reward guarantee is not hero pity. Future banner types must be checked against their own notices. The examples above are arithmetic deductions, not measured draw outcomes. For current windows use Current banners; for worked budgets and a pull-or-save decision use Lancelot or Elaine: pull advice.

Quick answers

Can an off-featured SSR reset pity?

Yes. It resets the SSR counter and activates the next-SSR featured guarantee. The banner bonus gauge is a separate measure.

Can I combine 60 bonus draws from each banner?

No. The 120th Draw Bonus belongs to the corresponding banner. Two gauges at 60 do not trigger one bonus.

Is a ten-pull guaranteed to contain an SSR?

The checked notices guarantee an SSR within 80 draws; they do not establish a separate SSR guarantee for each ten-pull. Do not substitute the skin draw’s maximum of 10 for this rule.
