Current Banners: Lancelot and Elaine

As checked on October 11, 2026, Lancelot and Elaine are the announced current Hero Pick Up choices. Both run from after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance, UTC.

Rules and content reviewed: October 11, 2026

Both require the main quest HP Recovery! and use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets. The banner notices end at maintenance, without giving an exact closing hour; check the corresponding maintenance notice before a last-day draw.

What rules are confirmed for both?

The full notices independently confirm an SSR within 80 draws, a 50% featured chance conditional on an SSR without the featured guarantee, and a guaranteed featured next SSR after an off-featured result. SSR count and featured-guarantee status carry to another Hero Pick Up. Each banner also has its own once-only featured bonus at 120 draws: an early pickup does not reset that gauge, and its progress does not carry to the other banner.

For how those rules affect spending, use the Lancelot-or-Elaine decision and budget guide. For mechanics use the pity guide; for switching use the carryover guide.

Source corrections and historical windows

Elaine’s official body was corrected on October 8 at 04:58 UTC to Whispers of the Wind, with a new event image. The article header still uses the earlier name Whisper of the Autumn Gale; these names refer to the same notice, not two current Elaine banners.

Khala and Selective Pick Up are September archives, not current choices after the October 8 boundary. The two checked October notices do not announce a successor after October 28. Use Upcoming banners for separately sourced future announcements.
