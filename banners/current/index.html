The Version 2.3 notice confirms increased featured-hero rates and Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets, but the individual in-game Rate Info panel remains the source for numeric rates, pity and guarantee details. The window ends at maintenance without an exact closing hour.

What rules are confirmed for both?

The full notices independently confirm an SSR within 80 draws, a 50% featured chance conditional on an SSR without the featured guarantee, and a guaranteed featured next SSR after an off-featured result. SSR count and featured-guarantee status carry to another Hero Pick Up. Each banner also has its own once-only featured bonus at 120 draws: an early pickup does not reset that gauge, and its progress does not carry to the other banner.

For the current decision, use the Estia-or-Clotho decision and budget guide. For mechanics use the pity guide; do not assume the older Version 2.2 numeric examples apply until the live Version 2.3 Rate Info is visible.
