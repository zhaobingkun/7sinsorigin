7DS Origin Banners: Availability, Rules and Budget

Check availability first, then price the reward you actually want. The two checked current banners are Lancelot and Elaine, ending before October 28 maintenance. This directory does not rank their combat strength.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

  • Lancelot — Heart-Piercing Eyes: quest eligibility, trial conditions and the distinction between hero and skin draws.
  • Elaine — Whispers of the Wind: the event name corrected by Netmarble on October 8 and Elaine’s own rules.

Both use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets, from after October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance, UTC. A later maintenance notice must establish the exact closing hour.

Record three things before spending

Record draws since your last SSR, whether your next SSR is guaranteed featured, and the selected banner’s bonus progress/claimed state. The two bonus gauges do not combine. Regular and skin tickets are not Hero Pick Up budget. An announcement confirms availability, not account-specific value.
