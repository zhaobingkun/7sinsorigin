Upcoming Banners

Use this page for planning, not certainty. Upcoming banner pages should stay close to official reveals, patch notes, and character pages so players can decide whether saving now makes more sense than pulling immediately.

Confirmed Next: Gowther

Gowther is the next confirmed Hero Pick Up banner. The official July 14 notice says Gowther Pick Up opens after the July 15 maintenance and runs until the August 5 maintenance. Elizabeth remains the current Version 1.6 pickup until the July 15 maintenance begins.

Gowther Pick Up Details · Version 1.7 Maintenance

Gowther Banner Rules

  • Uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.
  • Requires Main Quest HP Recovery!.
  • Guarantees at least one SSR within 80 draws.
  • If an SSR is not the pickup hero, the next SSR is guaranteed to be the pickup hero.
  • 120th Draw Bonus guarantees the pickup hero once on this banner, but that 120th bonus does not carry over to other Hero Pick Up events.

Save or Pull Logic

If Elizabeth does not solve a current account problem, saving through the final Version 1.6 window is now easier to justify because Gowther's banner, dates, and guarantee structure are confirmed. Wait for post-maintenance testing before calling Gowther mandatory for every roster.

Version 1.7 Context

Gowther arrives with Act 15, Liones Northern Front, Durak Boss Challenge, Deep Spider Nest Raid, a 14-day update check-in, Raid Missions, and Knighthood Boss Battle content. Banner planning should account for the new raid and support routes, not just character collection.
