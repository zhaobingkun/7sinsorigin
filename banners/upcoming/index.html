7DS Origin Next Banner: Confirmed vs Unannounced

The official notices checked on October 11 do not identify a successor after October 28. Lancelot and Elaine’s closing window does not establish the next hero, rates or rerun order.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

What counts as confirmation?

A formal Pick Up notice should identify the event, hero, start/end window and acquisition rules. A livestream preview, player poll, story appearance or older banner does not substitute for those fields. An old title saying “Pick Up” does not make the event active.

The checked notices are Lancelot and Elaine; use the tracker for their current window. A future format needs its own rules check.

How to plan without predicting a hero

  1. Count resources you already hold or can actually convert at the displayed in-game cost.
  2. Record Hero Pick Up SSR pity and featured-guarantee state; do not treat this banner’s 120-draw bonus as savings carried into a future one.
  3. If your current goal is complete, stop instead of “building pity” on a hero you would regret winning.
  4. When the next notice arrives, recalculate using its own tickets, rates, guarantees and deadline.

This is resource-management advice, not a future strength prediction. See worked draw budgets.
