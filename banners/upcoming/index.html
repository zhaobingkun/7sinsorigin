Upcoming Banners

Ban is now live after the completed August 26 maintenance. This page tracks what is confirmed after Ban and avoids presenting unannounced reruns as fact.

Ban Pick Up Is Live

Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw runs after the completed August 26 maintenance until the September 16 maintenance in UTC. The official notice confirms an increased Ban drop rate and Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

Ban Pick Up Notice · Current Banner Status

Ban Rules Confirmed

The Hero Pick Up confirms an SSR within 80 draws, a 50% Pick Up chance, and a guaranteed Pick Up hero on the next SSR if the Pick Up hero is missed. Accumulated draw count and Pick Up guarantee carry over to another Hero Pick Up; the 120th Draw Bonus is once per banner and does not carry over.

Ban Pick Up Rules · Ban Details

Current Planning Window

  • Ban Pick Up is live after maintenance and ends at the September 16 maintenance.
  • The August 26 maintenance completed after an extension; the final official window was 02:00–10:00 UTC.
  • Use the in-game Rate Info panel for detailed probability tables.
  • No post-Ban successor banner is officially confirmed yet.

Poll Draw Rerun Plan

Developer Notes #19 says the three heroes selected by the Poll Draw Survey are planned to appear at the same time during Version 2.1. Players will be able to choose and change their Pick Up selection, but the three names and exact timing are not yet stated in the notice.

Developer Notes #19 · Poll Draw Survey

Save or Pull Logic

If Ban is your priority, compare the live rules with your roster and use the Ban Trial Event before committing resources. If you are planning beyond Ban, save until another official pickup notice is published.
