Upcoming Banners

Ban remains live until the September 17 maintenance in UTC. This page tracks the confirmed Version 2.1 banners after maintenance and keeps later speculation separate.

Confirmed: Khala Pick Up

Khala Pick Up – A Grand Thief's Dignity opens after the September 17 maintenance and runs until the October 8 maintenance in UTC. The official notice confirms the 80-draw SSR guarantee, 50% Pick Up chance, carry-over rules, and a 120th Draw Bonus.

Khala Pick Up Notice · Khala Details

Confirmed: Selective Pick Up

Selective Pick Up – Stargazer Invitation opens after the same maintenance through the October 8 maintenance. Choose Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy; the other two are excluded from that draw, and the selected hero can be changed for the next draw.

Selective Pick Up Notice

Ban Until Maintenance

Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw remains the current Hero Pick Up until the September 17 maintenance. Its accumulated draw count and Pick Up guarantee carry over to another Hero Pick Up; the 120th Draw Bonus does not.

Ban Pick Up Rules · Current Banner Status

What Is Not Confirmed

No later successor beyond the confirmed Khala and Selective Pick Up notices is stated here. Treat future reruns, exact meta rankings, and unannounced timing as unconfirmed until Netmarble publishes them.
