Before your next draw

Read the counters on the banner you intend to use. Write down your draws since the last SSR, whether the next SSR guarantees the pickup hero, and progress toward that banner's 120th Draw Bonus.

Then set a ticket limit you can afford. An SSR guarantee and a pickup guarantee answer different questions; reaching one does not always mean receiving Lancelot.

Read Lancelot's confirmed rules · Investment priorities

How does the current Lancelot banner work?

Heart-Piercing Eyes runs from after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance, UTC. Its two progress systems have different carryover rules.

SSR pity and pickup guarantee

  • An SSR hero is guaranteed within 80 draws.
  • When you obtain an SSR without the pickup guarantee, it has a 50% chance to be Lancelot. This is conditional on an SSR, not a 50% chance on every draw.
  • If that SSR is not the pickup hero, the next SSR is guaranteed to be the pickup hero.
  • The SSR pity count and pickup guarantee carry over to the next Hero Pick Up banner.

The separate 120th Draw Bonus

Making 120 draws on this banner grants the pickup hero as a bonus, once per banner. Getting an SSR or the pickup hero early does not reset this bonus gauge.

The bonus gauge does not carry over. Do not add old banner draws to the current 120-draw gauge, or confuse this gauge with draws remaining until your next guaranteed SSR.

Source: Netmarble's Lancelot Pick Up notice. For Elaine, check her own notice and in-game counters before drawing.

What does that mean for my budget?

If your budget cannot reach your next SSR guarantee and the pickup guarantee is not active, be prepared to finish without Lancelot. If the next SSR is guaranteed to be the pickup hero, compare your remaining pity distance with the tickets you actually have. If you are considering the 120th Draw Bonus, count only this banner's bonus progress and decide whether the remaining cost is worthwhile after an early pickup.

Saving is a reasonable choice when your existing roster meets your next goal. A banner announcement alone does not establish that a hero is your best upgrade. Use the current banner budget and pull-or-save guide for the decision, and the current banner tracker for the active windows.

Choose an upgrade before another summon

Identify the stage, boss, or quest you cannot finish. Check your current team's weapons, equipment, and missing role before investing in another hero. A new character also needs materials; spreading upgrades across several unfinished builds can leave the original problem unsolved.

Quick answers before you play

Does 80 draws guarantee Lancelot?

It guarantees an SSR within 80 draws. Without the pickup guarantee, that SSR has a 50% chance to be Lancelot. The next SSR after a missed pickup is guaranteed to be the pickup hero; the 120th Draw Bonus is separate.

Should I chase the bonus after an early pickup?

An early pickup does not reset the bonus gauge, but it also does not make the remaining draws free. Compare the remaining cost with your next account goal before continuing.
