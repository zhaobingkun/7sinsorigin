Choose a safe troubleshooting path

  • Startup crash or failed entry: startup checks.
  • Steam controller or in-game input: layered controller checks.
  • Heat, frame drops, or mobile slowdown: mobile performance checks.

These are general diagnostics, not guaranteed fixes for every game issue. When an official notice matches your symptom, follow its explicit step before reinstalling or changing account settings.

When should I stop trying local fixes?

Make one reversible change at a time and record the result. Stop before deleting saves, unlinking accounts, repeating a payment, or installing unofficial patches. Contact official support with platform, version, exact error text, first occurrence, and reproduction steps; never send passwords or payment tokens.

Older issue states remain in the dated archive; an old resolved notice does not prove a new symptom is identical. Contact this site is for editorial corrections, not game support.
