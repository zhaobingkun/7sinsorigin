7DS Origin Version 2.4 Known Issues and Safe Troubleshooting

Check the official issue first, then try reversible local diagnostics. The current Version 2.4 notice lists Steam error 30021225-0, a party-leave movement issue and characters falling beneath the ground.

Editorial review: November 25, 2026. Official facts and analysis are identified separately.

Which Version 2.4 problems are still listed?

  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character elsewhere; reconnecting is the stated temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; a future update is planned.

Full 2.4 issue coverage · Official notice 381

Which issues were resolved?

The November 21 recommended update resolved the Act 1 Adventure Companion tutorial block, missing Ranking Boss challenge records, missing doors in the Cordin Reservoir Underground Waterway Administration Hall and heroes missing from Team Formation. It also fixed certain Elite Boss effect displays and improved Armor menu sorting.

The November 24 data patch performed server stabilization work; fully close and reconnect to download it.

Choose a safe troubleshooting path

  • Startup crash or failed entry: startup checks.
  • Steam controller or in-game input: layered controller checks.
  • Heat, frame drops or mobile slowdown: mobile performance checks.

These are general diagnostics, not guaranteed fixes for every game issue. Follow an official notice’s explicit step before reinstalling or changing account settings.

When should I stop local fixes?

Make one reversible change at a time. Stop before deleting saves, unlinking accounts, repeating a payment or installing unofficial patches. Contact official support with platform, version, exact error text and reproduction steps; never send passwords or payment tokens.

Older issue states remain in the dated archive; an old resolved notice does not prove a new symptom is identical.
