7DS Origin Known Issues and Safe Troubleshooting

Check the official issue first, then try reversible local diagnostics. An investigation is not a completed fix, and an older compensation notice does not apply to every new problem.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

Issues still listed by Netmarble

  • On iOS with a controller connected, the Timespace Junction: Extra event tab cannot be selected.
  • A cursor may intermittently disappear when entering Draw Bingo with a controller.
  • Hawk Pass Missions award an incorrect amount of XP.
  • Steam error 30021225-0: the notice says updating to the latest Steam build resolves it.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location moves the character elsewhere; reconnecting is the stated temporary workaround.
  • Characters may fall endlessly beneath the ground; a future update is planned, not a completed fix in this notice.

Coverage · Official issue notice (335). The official title is updated October 8 at 14:00 UTC. Our October 11 recheck is not a newly released patch.

Resolved and pending are different states

The October 8 patches addressed intermittent Knighthood Headquarters entry, the Act 17 “Leave It to Me!” tutorial blocker, and a specific Ferzen Mines Raid Tag Skill issue. Fully close and reconnect as directed by the patch notice before retrying.

The October 10 Knighthood unintended-score review is a separate notice with no final remedy announced. Headquarters access being fixed does not mean the ranking review is complete. Ranking review status · Patch details and sources

Choose a diagnostic route by symptom

  • Crash before or during entry: startup-crash checks.
  • Steam controller not detected or inputs failing: layered controller checks.
  • Mobile heat, stutter or degradation over a session: mobile performance checks.

These are general diagnostic procedures, not official fixes for every game bug. When an official notice matches the symptom, follow its specific directions first. Reinstalling locally cannot replace a pending server-side remedy.

When to stop and escalate

Make one reversible change at a time and record its effect. Stop before deleting saves, unlinking accounts, repeating payments or installing unofficial patches. Send official support the platform, version, exact error, first occurrence and reproduction steps—not passwords, payment data or full account tokens.

Older statuses stay in the dated archive. An old “resolved” label does not prove a new symptom has the same cause. Our Contact page is for article corrections, not game-account support.
