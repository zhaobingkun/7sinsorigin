7DS Origin Known Issues and Safe Troubleshooting

Check the official issue first, then try reversible local diagnostics. The October 19 notice adds a Chaos event mission-counting issue and retains the PS5 party-entry issue and confirms a mobile Merlin animation fix.

Editorial review: October 19, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

Issues still listed by Netmarble

  • In Chaos That Devours the Desert, SSR Equipment from the Draco King Drake Boss Challenge may not count for Event Mission V.
  • On PlayStation®5, [Party Introduction] → [Enter Manually] may fail when “Set to Searchable” is active.
  • Draco King Drake may give a lower Quality “Tyrant Dragon's Black Heart Top” at higher difficulty.
  • On iOS with a controller connected, the Timespace Junction: Extra event tab cannot be selected.
  • A cursor may intermittently disappear when entering Draw Bingo with a controller.
  • Hawk Pass missions award an incorrect amount of XP.
  • Steam error 30021225-0: updating to the latest Steam build is the stated workaround.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location moves the character elsewhere; reconnecting is the stated temporary workaround.
  • Characters may fall endlessly beneath the ground; a future update is planned.

Which problems were resolved?

The official update marks Merlin’s Wand Special Attack “Electromagnetic Field” animation interruption on mobile as resolved October 8. It also marks the Knighthood Boss Battle reset issue resolved October 13, plus intermittent inability to enter Knighthood Headquarters and the Act 17 tutorial popup resolved October 8. An October 8 server patch separately fixed a Ferzen Mines Raid Tag Skill issue.

Choose a safe troubleshooting path

  • Startup crash or failed entry: startup checks.
  • Steam controller or in-game input: layered controller checks.
  • Heat, frame drops, or mobile slowdown: mobile performance checks.

These are general diagnostics, not guaranteed fixes for every game issue. When an official notice matches your symptom, follow its explicit step before reinstalling or changing account settings.

When should I stop trying local fixes?

Make one reversible change at a time and record the result. Stop before deleting saves, unlinking accounts, repeating a payment, or installing unofficial patches. Contact official support with platform, version, exact error text, first occurrence, and reproduction steps; never send passwords or payment tokens.

Older issue states remain in the dated archive; an old resolved notice does not prove a new symptom is identical. Contact this site is for editorial corrections, not game support.
