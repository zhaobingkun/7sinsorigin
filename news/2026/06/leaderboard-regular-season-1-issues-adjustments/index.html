Leaderboard Regular Season 1 Issues and Content Adjustments

Published: June 30, 2026 · Checked: July 2, 2026

Direct answer: This June 30 notice is now historical. Netmarble fixed the buff carryover issue during the completed July 2 temporary maintenance. The [Abyss] Galland of Truth leaderboard was reset, Laurel of Glory rewards were distributed based on ranking status at the start of maintenance, and both Timespace Junction Sector 7 and Magic Pop opened after that maintenance.

Boss Challenge Leaderboard Measures

  • The affected Boss Challenge was [Abyss] Galland of Truth.
  • The July 2 temporary maintenance blocked buffs applied before entry from carrying over after entry.
  • Dishes are usable while waiting for the content to begin.
  • The affected leaderboard was reset during the temporary maintenance.
  • Laurel of Glory rewards were distributed based on ranking status at the start of maintenance.

Content Status After July 2

  • Timespace Junction Sector 7 opened after the July 2 temporary maintenance.
  • Magic Pop - The Day of Mischief Begins! opened after the July 2 temporary maintenance and runs until the July 15 maintenance.

What Players Should Do

Use this page for the original issue background, but use the July 2 maintenance notice for current fix status and compensation. For the event itself, use the Magic Pop event page.
