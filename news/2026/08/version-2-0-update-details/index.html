「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Update Details

Official update details published August 25, 2026 · reviewed August 26, 2026

Direct answer: Version 2.0 adds new hero Ban, the Ravens region, Main Quest Act 16, Beast of Destruction: Indura Monspeet, Transcendence Engravement/Refinement, new events, and a new ranking season. The official details also confirm that Ban is obtained from the Ban Pick Up draw.

What is new in Version 2.0?

  • Ban joins the roster, with the Righteous Outlaw Hero Pick Up.
  • Ravens and Main Quest Act 16, “The Traitor's Street and Star Fragments,” are added.
  • Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet adds Nightmare, Hell, Abyss, and Transcendence difficulties.
  • Transcendence Engravement/Refinement expands Engraved Equipment growth.
  • Raid Nightmare difficulty arrives for Taranis's Sanctuary and Deep Spider Nest, with Quality 120 equipment added to Hell rewards.
  • Party play receives an MVP display, and new Engraved Equipment is added for Howzer, Elizabeth, King, Gowther, Griamore, Derieri, and Bug.

Which events start after maintenance?

Ban Trial, A Gift from The Seven Deadly Sins, the Half-Anniversary Reward Selection Check-In, Lucky Snatch, Poll Draw Survey, and Echo of Jubilation begin after the August 26 maintenance. The Event Boss “Her Return,” Holy Knights and Thieves, Labyrinth, and Timespace Junction Season 2 are scheduled for later September maintenance windows.

What changes for new and existing players?

The tutorial Pick Up changes from SR Slater to SSR Meliodas for new accounts. Existing players receive SSR Meliodas by in-game mail after maintenance, or 180 Star Trace Crystalloids if Meliodas is already at Potential Tier 10. The update also adds crafting previews, remembers recent content difficulty selections, expands tutorials, and adds new recipes and pets.

What is the safe banner takeaway?

Ban's availability, event window, and increased drop rate are confirmed, but this notice does not provide a percentage rate, pity carryover rule, or universal meta verdict. Check the dedicated Ban Pick Up notice and test the hero before making a pull decision.
