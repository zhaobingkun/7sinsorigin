「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Schedule

Published: August 24, 2026 · Updated: August 26, 2026

Direct answer: Netmarble scheduled the Version 2.0 maintenance for August 26, 2026 from 02:00 to 09:00 UTC. Derieri remains the current banner until maintenance is completed; Ban is confirmed as the post-maintenance pickup subject.

What is confirmed?

  • Ban, Irreverent Holy Knight, Ravens, Indura Monspeet, Transcendence Engravement/Refinement, Ranking Season 4, and Raid [Nightmare].
  • Use Knighthood Boss Battle entries before maintenance because the entry count resets during the update.
  • Derieri-era events end around maintenance; Timespace Junction Season 1 continues through the September 2 maintenance.

What compensation is confirmed?

All players can claim Star Fragment x300 from the in-game mailbox after maintenance until September 9, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What should players read next?

The separate official update-details notice now covers the full Version 2.0 content list. The dedicated Ban Pick Up notice confirms the post-maintenance banner window and ticket type; numeric rates and pity carryover are not stated.

Official source: 「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Schedule.
