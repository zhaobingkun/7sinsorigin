「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Completed

Published: August 24, 2026 · Completed and updated: August 26, 2026 10:00 UTC

Direct answer: The Version 2.0 maintenance is completed and the game is available. The final official maintenance window was August 26, 2026 from 02:00 to 10:00 UTC after an extension.

What did the update add?

  • New SSR hero Ban and the Irreverent Holy Knight outfit skin.
  • Ravens, Act 16, Beast of Destruction Indura Monspeet, Transcendence Engravement/Refinement, Ranking Season 4, Raid [Nightmare], and new Engraved Equipment.
  • Ban Trial, Half-Anniversary events, Lucky Snatch, Poll Draw Survey, and Echo of Jubilation.

What compensation is confirmed?

The original maintenance compensation is Star Fragment x300, and the extension compensation adds another Star Fragment x300. Claim the available compensation from the in-game mailbox until September 9, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What changed from the pre-maintenance plan?

Netmarble extended the schedule from 09:00 to 10:00 UTC and marked this notice completed at 10:00 UTC. The official Version 2.0 details also extended the Chaos That Devours the Desert Hunting Event until the September 2 maintenance.

Official source: 「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Schedule.
