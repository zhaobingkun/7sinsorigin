「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Schedule

Published: August 24, 2026

Direct answer: Netmarble has scheduled maintenance for the 「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 update on August 26, 2026 (UTC). The detailed maintenance hours have not been announced yet, and the game will be unavailable during the maintenance. Derieri remains the current banner until maintenance is completed; Ban is confirmed as Version 2.0 content, not yet as a live current banner.

What is confirmed for Version 2.0?

  • New hero Ban and the exclusive skin Irreverent Holy Knight
  • New region Ravens
  • New Boss Challenge: Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet
  • New Transcendence Engravement/Refinement system
  • Ranking Season 4, To the Top
  • Raid [Nightmare] difficulty and new Engraved Equipment

What should players finish before maintenance?

The official notice says the Knighthood Boss Battle entry count will reset during maintenance. Use your remaining entries before the update so the replenished entries are available afterward. Derieri Pick Up, Derieri's exclusive skin draw and trial, the Half Anniversary Eve check-in, Chaos That Devours the Desert, A Journey to Find a Heart, Raid Missions, and Ranking Season 3 are all listed as ending around the August 26 maintenance. Timespace Junction Season 1 is listed through the September 2 maintenance.

What compensation is confirmed?

All players can claim Star Fragment x300 from the in-game mailbox after maintenance until September 9, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What is still unannounced?

Netmarble has not published the detailed maintenance hours or the separate Version 2.0 Update Details notice yet. This notice also does not confirm Ban banner rates, pity rules, or an exact pickup schedule, so those details should wait for the official update and banner notices.

Official source: 「The Traitor's Street」 Version 2.0 Maintenance Schedule.
