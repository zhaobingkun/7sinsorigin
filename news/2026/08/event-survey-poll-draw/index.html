Event Survey – Poll Draw Survey

Official Half-Anniversary event notice · reviewed August 26, 2026

Direct answer: The Poll Draw Survey runs after the August 26 maintenance until September 2, 2026 at 06:59:59 UTC. Eligible players can vote daily for three returning heroes from a six-hero candidate list.

Which heroes are candidates?

The official candidate list is Meliodas, Daisy, Escanor, Clotho, Merlin, and Elizabeth. The survey is a vote for a possible return; it is not a confirmation that any individual hero will receive a rerun banner.

How does daily voting work?

  • Vote once per day for three heroes.
  • On Day 1, choose three heroes and one of those three for one extra vote.
  • From Day 2, after voting, choose one random hero from the overall results and move one vote to a hero of your choice.
  • Vote counts reset daily at 07:00 UTC, and results combine all regions.
  • Votes cannot be cancelled or changed after submission.

Who can participate?

Voting is available to accounts that have completed Netmarble Login. Check the official notice and in-game survey screen for the live participation flow; the poll itself does not establish a future banner date or rate.

Official source: Event Survey - Poll Draw Survey.
