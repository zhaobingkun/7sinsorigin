Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw and Exclusive Skin

Official Version 2.0 banner notice · Published August 25, 2026 · live after completed August 26 maintenance

Direct answer: Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw is live after the completed August 26 maintenance and runs until the September 16 maintenance in UTC. It increases the drop rate for SSR Ban and uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

What are the confirmed Hero Pick Up rules?

  • An SSR hero is guaranteed within 80 draws.
  • The Pick Up hero has a 50% chance to appear when the SSR guarantee is reached.
  • If the Pick Up hero does not appear, the next SSR hero is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.
  • Accumulated draw count and the guaranteed Pick Up hero status carry over to another Hero Pick Up.
  • The 120th Draw Bonus guarantees the Pick Up hero once per banner. Its gauge does not reset when the hero appears early, but the bonus does not carry over to another Pick Up.
  • Use the in-game Rate Info panel for the detailed probability table.

What does Ready for Battle include?

The exclusive-skin draw includes Ban's Irreverent Holy Knight Outfit Skin and Demon Cudgel Vesta Weapon Skin. The first draw is free; later draws use Exclusive Skin Draw Tickets. Rewards do not duplicate and all rewards are guaranteed within a maximum of 10 draws. Skins change appearance only and do not affect stats or skill effects.

Should you pull?

Use the fully upgraded Ban Trial Event first, then compare Ban with your account's missing roles. The official rules are clear, but they do not establish a universal meta ranking or a must-pull verdict.
