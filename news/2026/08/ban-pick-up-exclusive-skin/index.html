Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw and Exclusive Skin

Official Version 2.0 banner notice · reviewed August 26, 2026

Direct answer: Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw starts after the August 26 maintenance and runs until the September 16 maintenance in UTC. It increases the drop rate for SSR Ban and uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

Ban Pick Up rules confirmed

  • Featured hero: [SSR] Ban.
  • Banner period: after August 26 maintenance through the September 16 maintenance, UTC.
  • Draw currency: Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.
  • Official text confirms an increased Ban drop rate but does not state a numeric percentage in the notice.

Ready for Battle exclusive skin

The Ready for Battle draw includes Ban's Irreverent Holy Knight Outfit Skin and Demon Cudgel Vesta Weapon Skin. All rewards, including those skins, are guaranteed within 10 draws. The first draw is free; later draws use Exclusive Skin Draw Tickets. Skins change appearance only and do not change stats or skill effects.

Should you pull immediately?

Wait until the banner is live and inspect the in-game rules. The notice confirms timing and ticket types, but it does not confirm pity carryover, a numeric rate, or a universal must-pull verdict. Compare Ban with your roster and try the fully upgraded Ban Trial Event before committing resources.
