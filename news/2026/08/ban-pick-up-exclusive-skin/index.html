Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw and Exclusive Skin

Published August 25, 2026 · Updated September 15 · live until the September 17 maintenance in UTC

Direct answer: Ban Pick Up is the current Hero Pick Up until the September 17 maintenance. It increases the SSR Ban rate and uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

What are the confirmed rules?

  • An SSR hero is guaranteed within 80 draws.
  • The Pick Up hero has a 50% chance at the SSR guarantee.
  • If missed, the next SSR hero is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.
  • Accumulated draw count and Pick Up guarantee carry over to another Hero Pick Up.
  • The 120th Draw Bonus guarantees the hero once per banner and does not carry over.

What does the exclusive skin draw include?

Ready for Battle includes Ban's Irreverent Holy Knight Outfit Skin and Demon Cudgel Vesta Weapon Skin. The first draw is free, rewards do not duplicate, and the full pool is guaranteed within 10 draws. Skins change appearance only and do not affect stats or skill effects.

Should you pull?

Try the fully upgraded Ban Trial Event first and compare the role with your account. The official notice does not establish a universal must-pull verdict.
