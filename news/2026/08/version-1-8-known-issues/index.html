Version 1.8 Known Issues Updated August 26

Published: August 5, 2026 · Updated: August 26, 2026 10:00 UTC

Direct answer: The official Version 1.8 Known Issues notice was updated on August 26. The current list covers the Hermit Beneath the Sand entry label, controller cursor, Steam, party-location, and falling-through-ground issues.

What remains unresolved?

  • The Hermit Beneath the Sand entry screen may omit the quest name; clear Beings of Deep Darkness in Chaos That Devours the Desert to enter.
  • Using a controller to view a Set Effect and selecting Back can make the cursor disappear.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 can prevent access; update the Steam build.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location can move the character elsewhere; reconnect as a temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; the official notice says a future update will address it.

What was resolved on August 26?

  • Swiftest Showdown Day 4 retry completion not registering.
  • Previous Season leaderboard personal records not showing the Top % text.

What should you do?

Apply the latest game update, reconnect, and retry the affected activity. For Version 2.0 issues, use the newer Version 2.0 Known Issues page.

Official source: Version 1.8 Known Issues.
