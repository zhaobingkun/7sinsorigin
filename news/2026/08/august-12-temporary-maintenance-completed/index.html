August 12 Temporary Maintenance Completed

Published: August 12, 2026 · Updated: August 14, 2026 09:37 UTC

Direct answer: The August 12 temporary maintenance completed at 08:00 UTC after the schedule was extended from 07:40 UTC. The game is available again, and the official notice grants Star Fragment x300 compensation until August 26 at 14:59 UTC. The notice was updated on August 14 to record a still-unresolved Swiftest Showdown Day 4 retry-registration issue.

What opened?

  • Half Anniversary Eve Check-In Event
  • Chaos That Devours the Desert Hunting Event
  • Rewinding Fate - Into the Memory of Dimensions
  • Timespace Junction Sector 11

What did the maintenance fix?

The official notice lists fixes for Guardian Golem retry and ranking behavior, equipment passive cooldowns, Deep Spider Nest, solo raid retry activation, Timespace Sector 9 mechanics, quest rewards and progression, Path of Exploration, event mission navigation, Battle Support Mode, shop routing, armor sorting, warp-point HP recovery, field fragments, burst requirements, reward quantity text, Potential UI, Special Codex hero graphics, and Derieri's Wild Rush cooldown display.

What was added on August 14?

The updated notice records that clearing the Day 4 mission after retrying in Swiftest Showdown - Time Attack Challenge! may occasionally fail to register as cleared. Netmarble says the issue will be addressed in a future update; it is also tracked on the Version 1.8 Known Issues page.

Important compensation timing

Claim the Star Fragment x300 maintenance compensation from the in-game mailbox by August 26 at 14:59 UTC. Players affected by the Guardian Golem ranking reset are also due Star Fragment x1,000, claimable through the same deadline.

Official source: August 12 (Wed) Temporary Maintenance [Completed].
