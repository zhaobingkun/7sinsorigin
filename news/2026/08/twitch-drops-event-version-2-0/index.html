Version 2.0 Twitch Drops Event: Watch-Time Rewards

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: August 26, 2026 10:50 UTC · Event window: after the August 26 maintenance to September 21, 2026 14:59 UTC

Direct answer: The Version 2.0 Twitch Drops Event runs after the August 26 maintenance through September 21, 2026 at 14:59 UTC. Link your game account and Twitch account, watch Drops-enabled streams in the official game category, claim the reward in Twitch Drops Inventory and on the official event page, then check the in-game mailbox.

What are the watch-time rewards?

  • 15 minutes: Supreme Mastery EXP x10.
  • 30 minutes: Special Refinement Stone x10.
  • 1 hour: Special Tier Up Stone x10.
  • 1 hour 30 minutes: Small Cube Key Bundle x3.
  • 2 hours: Regular Hero Draw Ticket x5.

How do you participate?

  1. Log in to your Seven Deadly Sins: Origin game account.
  2. Log in to the Twitch account you want to link; the official page links the accounts automatically.
  3. Watch streams in the The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin Twitch category with Drops enabled.
  4. Claim the Drop in Twitch Drops Inventory, then claim it on the official event page for mailbox delivery.

What should you know before claiming?

  • Rewards are delivered as [Reward] Coupon Reward items to the in-game mailbox.
  • Rewards go to the character on the first server you access after linking accounts.
  • After the first reward claim, you cannot change the linked account for the same campaign.
  • Drops expire if they remain unclaimed in Twitch Drops Inventory for 10 days.
  • Rewards claimed on the event page remain in the in-game mailbox for 7 days.
