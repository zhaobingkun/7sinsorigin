Half-Anniversary Festival Livestream Replay and Version 2.0 Preview

Published: August 18, 2026 · Official notice updated: August 21, 2026 · Stream time: August 21, 2026 11:00 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble’s August 21 notice linked the Half-Anniversary Festival Special Livestream for August 21, 2026 at 11:00 UTC. The official notice says it previews major updates coming in Version 2.0 and explains Half-Anniversary events and rewards; it also says a replay will be available after the stream.

When was the official livestream?

  • Date: August 21, 2026.
  • Time: 11:00 UTC.
  • Channels: Korean, Japanese, and Global streams were planned simultaneously.
  • Replay: Use the official YouTube broadcast link below for the replay route.

What will the stream show?

The confirmed highlights are new character Ban skills and gameplay, a new main quest, and new content. The notice also describes the broadcast as a first look at major Version 2.0 updates, alongside Half-Anniversary event and reward details.

Where can I watch the replay?

Watch the official Half-Anniversary Festival livestream replay on YouTube. The link is taken from Netmarble’s August 21 official announcement.

What is not confirmed yet?

This is a preview schedule, not a complete Version 2.0 patch note. It does not confirm a Ban release date, banner rates, pity rules, or the next Hero Pick Up. Keep Derieri as the current confirmed banner until a separate official notice changes that routing.
