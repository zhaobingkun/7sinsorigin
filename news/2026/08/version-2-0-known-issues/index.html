Version 2.0 Known Issues Updated August 31

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: August 31, 2026 08:02 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's official Version 2.0 Known Issues notice was updated on August 31. It now tracks the Ban-owned Protein Bug Skewer region-quest problem, an Orb of Greed reset, Act 16 and UI issues, and Steam/controller troubleshooting. Physical damage-number display and download-screen logo-language issues were resolved on August 31; two PlayStation 5 issues were resolved on August 26.

Current issues

  • Ban and Protein Bug Skewer: the Region Quest for cooking Protein Bug Skewer cannot be cleared while owning Ban. Netmarble added this issue on August 31.
  • Orb of Greed reset: event currency from Lucky Snatch was reset. Netmarble says the reset currency will be redistributed; check the official notice for the latest details.
  • Bug's Normal Skill “Dark Thrash” may show no effect during Act 16 story battles.
  • Certain puzzles may not progress after summoning a pet.
  • An incorrect message may appear in Act 16 “Into the Basement” when progressing with a pet summoned.
  • Oasis Village NPCs may appear as treasure-chest icons on the minimap.
  • The Hermit Beneath the Sand entry screen may not display its quest-name requirement; clear Beings of Deep Darkness in Chaos That Devours the Desert to enter.
  • Using a controller to view an Engraved Equipment Set Effect and selecting Back may make the cursor disappear.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move a character to another area; reconnecting is the temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; Netmarble says this will be addressed in a future update.

Resolved on August 31

  • Physical-attribute attacks intermittently failing to show damage numbers.
  • The download screen showing the wrong logo language after connecting to the game.

Resolved on August 26

  • Act 16 “To Grow Stronger” quest progression failing when talking to Daisy on PlayStation 5.
  • Entering near the entrance of The Hermit Beneath the Sand being impossible on PlayStation 5.

What should you do?

Fully exit the game, apply the August 31 recommended update, reconnect, and retry the affected content. For Orb of Greed compensation, keep the official notice bookmarked and check its redistribution details. Use the Bugs and Errors hub for broader setup and crash troubleshooting.

Official source: Version 2.0 Known Issues.
