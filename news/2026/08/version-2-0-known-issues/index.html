Version 2.0 Known Issues Updated September 15

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: September 15, 2026 10:11 UTC

Direct answer: The official notice now tracks incorrect end dates for the Transcendence versions of Her Return and Indura Monspeet, plus the Oasis Village minimap, Ban-owned Protein Bug Skewer, Act 16 pet-message, controller, Steam, party, and falling-under-ground issues.

Current issues

  • Event end-date display: Her Return Transcendence is available until September 23 at 06:59:59 UTC; Indura Monspeet Transcendence is available until the September 17 maintenance.
  • Oasis Village minimap: NPCs may appear as treasure-chest icons; a later fix is planned.
  • Ban and Protein Bug Skewer: the Region Quest cannot be cleared while owning Ban; resolution is scheduled for the September 17 maintenance.
  • An incorrect message may appear in Act 16 “Into the Basement” with a pet summoned.
  • Controller navigation may lose the cursor after viewing an Engraved Equipment Set Effect.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may block access; update Steam to the latest build.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move a character; reconnect as a temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; a future update is planned.

Resolved items

September 3 resolved four quest, puzzle, and event-entry issues. August 31 resolved the Physical damage-number display and download-screen logo-language issues. August 26 resolved two PlayStation 5 Act 16 and dungeon-entry issues.

What should you do?

Fully exit, update, reconnect, and retry. Follow the official workaround where provided, and use the Bugs and Errors hub for broader troubleshooting.
