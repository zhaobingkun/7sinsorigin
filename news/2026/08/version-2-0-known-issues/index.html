Version 2.0 Known Issues Updated August 26

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: August 26, 2026 11:03 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's official Version 2.0 Known Issues notice currently tracks an Orb of Greed reset, several Act 16 and UI issues, physical damage-number display problems, and Steam/controller troubleshooting. The notice says reset Orbs of Greed will be redistributed and marks two PlayStation 5 issues resolved on August 26.

Current issues

  • Orb of Greed reset: event currency from Lucky Snatch was reset. Orbs can currently be earned normally, and Netmarble says the reset currency will be redistributed after further details.
  • Physical-attribute attacks may intermittently fail to show damage numbers.
  • Bug's Normal Skill “Dark Thrash” may show no effect during Act 16 story battles.
  • Certain puzzles may not progress after summoning a pet.
  • An incorrect message may appear in Act 16 “Into the Basement” when progressing with a pet summoned.
  • Oasis Village NPCs may appear as treasure-chest icons on the minimap.
  • The download screen may show the wrong logo language after connecting to the game.
  • The Hermit Beneath the Sand entry screen may not display its quest-name requirement; clear Beings of Deep Darkness in Chaos That Devours the Desert to enter.
  • Using a controller to view an Engraved Equipment Set Effect and selecting Back may make the cursor disappear.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move a character to another area; reconnecting is the temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; Netmarble says this will be addressed in a future update.

Resolved on August 26

  • Act 16 “To Grow Stronger” quest progression failing when talking to Daisy on PlayStation 5.
  • Entering near the entrance of The Hermit Beneath the Sand being impossible on PlayStation 5.

What should you do?

Apply the latest client update, reconnect, and retry the affected content. For Orb of Greed compensation, keep the official notice bookmarked and wait for the redistribution details. Use the Bugs and Errors hub for broader setup and crash troubleshooting.

Official source: Version 2.0 Known Issues.
