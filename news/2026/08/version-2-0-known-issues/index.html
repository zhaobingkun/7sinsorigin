Version 2.0 Known Issues Archive Updated September 17

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: September 17, 2026 09:01 UTC · Historical version record

Archive note: This page records the final tracked Version 2.0 issue state. For active Version 2.1 troubleshooting, use the Version 2.1 Known Issues notice.

Issues still listed at the Version 2.0 boundary

  • Controller navigation could lose the cursor after viewing a material Set Effect.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 could block access; updating the Steam build was the official workaround.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location could move the character; reconnecting was a temporary workaround.
  • Characters could occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; a future update was planned.

Issues resolved by September 17

The official record marked the Her Return and Indura Monspeet end-date display, Oasis Village minimap icons, Ban-owned Protein Bug Skewer quest, Act 16 pet message, event daily resets, and several earlier quest, puzzle, UI, and PS5 issues resolved.

Official source: Version 2.0 Known Issues.
