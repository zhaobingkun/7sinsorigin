Version 2.0 Known Issues Updated September 3

Published: August 26, 2026 · Updated: September 3, 2026 09:20 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's official Version 2.0 Known Issues notice was updated on September 3. It now tracks the Ban-owned Protein Bug Skewer region-quest problem, an Act 16 pet-message issue, controller, Steam, party, and falling-under-ground problems. Five quest, puzzle, minimap, and event-entry issues were resolved by the September 3 maintenance; the two August 31 display issues and two PlayStation 5 issues remain resolved.

Current issues

  • Ban and Protein Bug Skewer: the Region Quest for cooking Protein Bug Skewer cannot be cleared while owning Ban. Netmarble added this issue on August 31.
  • An incorrect message may appear in Act 16 “Into the Basement” when progressing with a pet summoned.
  • Using a controller to view an Engraved Equipment Set Effect and selecting Back may make the cursor disappear.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move a character to another area; reconnecting is the temporary workaround.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; Netmarble says this will be addressed in a future update.

Resolved on September 3

  • Bug's Normal Skill “Dark Thrash” effect not displaying during Act 16 story battles.
  • Certain puzzles not progressing after summoning a pet.
  • Oasis Village NPCs displaying as treasure-chest icons on the minimap.
  • The Collapsed Sand side-quest chest changing regions and becoming locked after its puzzle was completed.
  • The Hermit Beneath the Sand entry screen not displaying its quest-name requirement.

Resolved on August 31

  • Physical-attribute attacks intermittently failing to show damage numbers.
  • The download screen showing the wrong logo language after connecting to the game.

Resolved on August 26

  • Act 16 “To Grow Stronger” quest progression failing when talking to Daisy on PlayStation 5.
  • Entering near the entrance of The Hermit Beneath the Sand being impossible on PlayStation 5.

What should you do?

Fully exit the game, apply the September 3 maintenance update, reconnect, and retry the affected content. If the problem is still listed above, follow the official workaround where one is provided. Use the Bugs and Errors hub for broader setup and crash troubleshooting.
