Seven Deadly Sins Origin Partner Creator Program Season 2

Published: August 10, 2026 · Official creator notice

Direct answer: Netmarble opened recruitment for Partner Creator Program Season 2 on August 10, 2026 at 09:30 UTC. Applications remain open until further notice, and Season 1 Partner Creator activities end as Season 2 begins.

Who can apply?

The official notice requires at least 1,000 subscribers or followers on the applicant's primary platform and the ability to create and publish The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin-related content. Season 1 participants may apply again. Selection also depends on Netmarble's internal review and criteria, so meeting the threshold does not guarantee acceptance.

What activity is required?

Applicants need to meet at least one of the following monthly activity options:

  • Upload at least four edited long-form videos per month, each at least five minutes long.
  • Upload at least six edited short-form videos per month, each at least 30 seconds long.
  • Host at least four live streams per month, each at least one hour long, with the replay available.

What benefits does the program offer?

Netmarble lists regular in-game currency support, production support such as coupons for subscriber or livestream events when a content proposal is submitted, an official Discord Partner Creator role and channel, invitations to offline events, and possible promotion through official channels.

How do I apply?

Complete the official Partner Creator Program application form. The notice says the schedule and details may change, and selected creators will receive further program information at the email address used in the survey.

Important creator-policy notes

Videos must stay publicly available during the activity period, and creators must disclose paid advertising or sponsorship. The official notice also says eligibility can be restricted or revoked for policy violations. This program announcement does not confirm any new in-game banner, character, reward rate, or gameplay change.
