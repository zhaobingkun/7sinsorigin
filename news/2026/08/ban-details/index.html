Hero '[SSR] Ban' Details

Official Version 2.0 character notice · reviewed August 26, 2026 06:12 UTC

Direct answer: Ban is the new SSR hero for Version 2.0. The official update details connect him to the Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw draw, which uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets and runs after the August 26 maintenance until the September 16 maintenance in UTC.

What does the official notice confirm?

  • Rarity: SSR.
  • Weapons: Nunchaku, Greatsword, and Gauntlets.
  • Acquisition: the Ban Pick Up – The Righteous Outlaw event.
  • Ban unlocks eight upgraded cooking recipes through the Supreme Feast Adventure Skill after acquisition.

What is not confirmed here?

The official character notice does not establish a complete meta ranking, exact skill numbers, pull probability, pity carryover, or whether Ban is mandatory for future content. Use the in-game skill descriptions and live testing for those decisions.
