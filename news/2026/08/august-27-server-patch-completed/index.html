August 27 Server Patch: Equipment, Essence, and Act 16 Fixes

Published: August 27, 2026 · Patch completed: August 27 10:32 UTC · Updated: August 27 10:33 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble completed the August 27 server patch at 10:32 UTC. It fixed three Version 2.0 problems: higher-grade equipment being consumed during Engraved Equipment Refinement, missing Transcendence Essence drops in the Indura Monspeet Boss Challenge, and balance or HP handling in two Act 16 quests.

What was the patch schedule?

The official patch ran from 10:20 to 10:32 UTC on August 27. Players could be temporarily disconnected and should reconnect so the patch applies properly.

What happened to consumed equipment?

The patch fixed the issue that could consume higher-grade equipment as refinement material. Engraved Equipment consumed before this patch is scheduled to be restored during the September 2 maintenance. Players who need restoration sooner should contact Customer Support.

What changed for Indura Monspeet?

Transcendence Essence should now drop after defeating the Boss Challenge “Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet” on Abyss and Transcendence difficulties.

What changed in Act 16?

Netmarble adjusted “Furious Ban” and “Going From Bad to Worse” by decreasing Ban’s Attack. The HP of the story-exclusive hero Bug is also restored when entering “Going From Bad to Worse” after completing the battle in “Furious Ban”.

What should players do?

Reconnect after the patch, then retry the affected refinement, Boss Challenge, or Act 16 quest. For other Version 2.0 problems, check the official Known Issues coverage; the patch does not mean every listed issue is resolved.
