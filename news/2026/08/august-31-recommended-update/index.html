August 31 Recommended Update: Two Version 2.0 Fixes

Published: August 31, 2026 · Update scheduled: August 31, 2026 02:00 UTC · Updated: August 31, 2026 02:00 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's August 31 Recommended Update fixed two Version 2.0 issues: physical-attribute attacks intermittently failing to show damage numbers and the post-connection download screen showing the wrong logo language.

What did the August 31 update fix?

  • Physical-attribute attacks now have a fix for the intermittent missing damage-number display.
  • The download screen shown after connecting to the game now has a fix for incorrect logo-language display.

Was there maintenance?

No. This was a recommended update applied without maintenance. Netmarble scheduled it for August 31 at 02:00 UTC, with availability applied sequentially depending on the platform.

What should players do?

Fully exit the game, install the latest update on your platform or device, and refresh or try again if the update does not appear. Reconnect before checking either fixed issue.

What remains in the Version 2.0 issue list?

The separate official Version 2.0 Known Issues notice still lists the Ban-owned Protein Bug Skewer region-quest problem, Act 16 and pet-related issues, the Hermit Beneath the Sand entry text issue, Steam error 30021225-0, party movement, and falling beneath the ground. The August 31 update does not mean every issue is resolved.
