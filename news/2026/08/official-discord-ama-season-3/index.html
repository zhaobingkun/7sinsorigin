Official Discord AMA Season 3: Questions and Schedule

Published: August 21, 2026 · Question window: August 21 to September 2, 2026 06:59 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble’s official Discord AMA Season 3 opened its question period on August 21, 2026 at 11:00 UTC. Players can submit questions until September 2, 2026 at 06:59 UTC in the official ama-questions-season3 channel.

How do I submit a question?

Join the official Seven Deadly Sins: Origin Discord, then post your question in the ama-questions-season3 channel. The development team says it will review and answer submitted questions.

What is the AMA schedule?

  • Question submission: August 21, 2026 11:00 UTC to September 2, 2026 06:59 UTC.
  • Answers: questions will be answered sequentially through a notice, starting with those reviewed first.
  • The official notice does not promise a single post containing every answer.

Which topics can I ask about?

Netmarble lists five question categories: gameplay and mechanics; community and platforms; content; suggestions and requests; and other topics.

What should participants know?

  • Not every question can be answered. Questions that are too vague or contain inappropriate content may not be addressed.
  • Submitted questions may be used in future official content.
  • After the AMA ends, the channel will be view-only and chat will be restricted.
  • Unrelated or intentionally disruptive messages may be removed without prior notice.

Does the AMA confirm new characters or banners?

No. The AMA notice opens a question channel but does not confirm a new hero, banner, rate, pity rule, or release date. Treat those details as unconfirmed until Netmarble publishes them separately.
