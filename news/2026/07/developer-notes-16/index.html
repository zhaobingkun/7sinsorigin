Developer Notes #16: Version 1.7 New Raid, Knighthood Boss Battle, and QoL

Published: July 13, 2026 · Official source: Developer Notes #16

Direct answer: Developer Notes #16 confirms several Version 1.7 additions: weapon-specific equipment presets, a Combat View Assist setting, the new Deep Spider Nest Raid, a one-time Mystic Key recharge for players who use their current keys before the update, Knighthood Boss Battle with weekly Knighthood Rankings, and a 100-day thank-you gift. It does not confirm banner rates, pity, or a complete Gowther banner format.

Version 1.7 QoL changes

  • Weapon-specific presets will let a hero's Engraved Equipment, Armor, and Accessories change automatically based on the weapon currently in use.
  • The new Combat View Assist option is designed to prioritize monster and party-member silhouettes when skill or Combined Attack effects obscure the battlefield.
  • The Armor registered to each weapon preset will not have its stats combined across presets, and equipment shared with another hero still moves away from the original hero.

New raid: Deep Spider Nest

Deep Spider Nest is returning as a new Raid built from the original dungeon structure, with new areas and routes. Elite Monsters will appear in random locations within a designated area, and players need to locate and defeat every Elite Monster to earn all available rewards. The final boss Shakeera returns with familiar and new patterns, and the raid will have its own Pledge system.

Mystic Key recharge note

Because the Version 1.7 update is scheduled in the middle of the week, Netmarble plans to recharge 2 Mystic Keys for Star Guardians who have already used their current keys. The official note says this is a recharge of used keys, not an extra distribution, so players who want the recharge should use all current Mystic Keys before the update.

Knighthood Boss Battle and rankings

Knighthood Boss Battles are planned to open the week after the Version 1.7 update. Members can participate solo or in a party, score by dealing damage within a time limit, and face stronger bosses after a boss HP bar is depleted. Weekly Knighthood Rankings will use the Knighthood's highest individual score rather than the combined score of every member.

100-day thank-you gift

  • Star Fragment x1,000.
  • Radiant Potion of Charm x2.
  • Fruit and Meat Pie x30.
  • Claim deadline: August 5, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.
