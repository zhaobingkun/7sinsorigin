July 2 Temporary Maintenance Completed: Fixes, Compensation, Sector 7, and Magic Pop

Published: July 2, 2026 · Checked: July 2, 2026

Direct answer: The July 2 temporary maintenance is complete and the game is available again. The final maintenance window was 05:00-09:30 UTC. Compensation includes Star Fragment x300, plus extension compensation of Star Fragment x300 and Random Avatar Chest x1, claimable from the in-game mailbox until July 16 at 14:59 UTC.

Main Fixes

  • Fixed buff effects from skills, food, and similar sources carrying over after entering Dungeons, Boss Challenges, Timespace Junction, or Raids.
  • Reset Boss Challenge [Abyss] Galland of Truth rankings, while Laurel of Glory rewards are based on ranking status at the start of maintenance.
  • Fixed Quality over 130 equipment refining, PS5 Memories in the Spray photo quest progress, and missing A Midsummer Day's Dream event quests.
  • Fixed several Raid, Equipment Refinement, Banakro, dungeon waiting-area food, and Taranis's Sanctuary Hell time-limit issues.

Content Opened

The official maintenance notice lists Timespace Junction Sector 7 and Magic Pop - The Day of Mischief Begins as content opened through this maintenance. The earlier delay wording should now be treated as resolved.
