Magic Pop - The Day of Mischief Begins: Tokens, Avatars, and Mission Rewards

Published: July 2, 2026 · Checked: July 2, 2026

Direct answer: Magic Pop - The Day of Mischief Begins runs from after the July 2 temporary maintenance until the July 15 maintenance. The official requirement is completing the main quest Calm Days. Play event quests and missions to earn Regular Hero Draw Tickets, Magic Pop Tokens, avatar rewards, Star Fragments, emojis, and the Sharpshooter title.

Quest Rewards

  • Completing all quests can award Regular Hero Draw Ticket x10.
  • Quest rewards also include Magic Pop Token x350, Special Mastery EXP x20, and Gold x200,000.
  • Event quests unlock sequentially from Day 1 through Day 3.

Mission Rewards

  • Phase I includes Random Avatar Chest x1, Magic Pop Token x125, and Star Fragment x150.
  • Phase II includes Star Fragment x150, Magic Pop Token x250, and a final Magic Pop Token x250 reward.
  • Phase III includes Star Fragment x200, Magic Pop Token x375, and Hawk Emoji 01/02.
  • Phase IV includes Star Fragment x300, Magic Pop Token x700, and the Sharpshooter title.

Exchange Shop Notes

Magic Pop Tokens can be exchanged for rewards such as a Seven Deadly Sins Avatar Chest, Brilliant Engraving Seal, SSR Magic Crystal Shards, Hawk Emoji 03, Large Cube Key Bundles, armor materials, Platinum Ingots, Special Enhancement Stones, Special Refinement Stones, Special Mastery EXP, and Gold. Unused Magic Pop Tokens reset after the event ends.
