Twitch Drops Event: Version 1.7 Watch-Time Rewards

Published: July 13, 2026 · Event window: after July 15 update maintenance to August 9, 2026 14:59 UTC

Direct answer: The Version 1.7 Twitch Drops Event runs from after the July 15, 2026 update maintenance until August 9, 2026 at 14:59 UTC. Link your The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin game account with your Twitch account, watch streams in the official game category with Drops enabled, claim rewards in Twitch Drops Inventory and on the event page, then check the in-game mailbox.

Watch-time rewards

  • 15 minutes: Supreme Mastery EXP x10.
  • 30 minutes: Special Refinement Stone x10.
  • 1 hour: Special Tier Up Stone x10.
  • 1 hour 30 minutes: Cube Key Bundle x3.
  • 2 hours: Regular Hero Draw Ticket x3.

How to participate

  • Log in to your game account and the Twitch account you want to link.
  • Visit the official Drops event page and connect the accounts before watching.
  • Watch Twitch streams under the The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin category with Drops enabled.
  • Claim Drops from Twitch Drops Inventory, then claim rewards on the event page for mailbox delivery.

Important claim notes

  • Drops are delivered as [Reward] Coupon Reward items in the in-game mailbox.
  • Rewards go to the character on the first server accessed after linking accounts.
  • After claiming the first reward with linked accounts, the linked account cannot be changed for the same campaign.
  • Drops expire if not claimed within 10 days of becoming available in Twitch Drops Inventory.
  • Rewards sent from the event page are stored in the in-game mailbox for 7 days.
