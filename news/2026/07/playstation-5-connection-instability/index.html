PlayStation 5 Connection Instability Notice

Published: July 24, 2026 12:35 UTC · Official status notice

Direct answer: Netmarble posted a PlayStation 5 connection instability notice on July 24, 2026 at 12:35 UTC. The issue affects the PlayStation 5 platform, and the cause is still under investigation. The official notice does not yet mark the issue as resolved.

Current status

The notice says the team has identified connection instability on PlayStation 5 and will update the same notice after the issue is resolved. There is no compensation, maintenance window, or workaround listed in this initial notice.

What should PS5 players do?

  • Treat this as an active platform-status issue until Netmarble posts a resolved update.
  • Do not confuse it with the earlier Europe server notice, which was resolved on July 16.
  • If you can reach the game on another platform, keep PS5-specific connection symptoms separate from account or Steam errors.
