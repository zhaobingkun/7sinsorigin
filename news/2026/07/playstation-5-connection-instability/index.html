PlayStation 5 Connection Instability Resolved

Published: July 24, 2026 12:35 UTC · Resolved: July 25, 2026 02:20 UTC · Official status notice

Direct answer: Netmarble marked the PlayStation 5 connection instability issue as resolved on July 25, 2026 at 02:20 UTC. The game is now available for normal play on PlayStation 5 according to the official update.

Current status

The notice was first published on July 24 at 12:35 UTC and updated on July 25 at 02:32 UTC after the issue was resolved. It is a platform-status update, not a maintenance notice or compensation notice.

What should PS5 players do?

  • Try reconnecting normally now that the official notice is resolved.
  • If connection problems continue, restart the client and check whether your issue matches an active Version 1.7 known issue.
  • Keep PS5-specific connection symptoms separate from Steam error 30021225-0 and the earlier Europe server notice.
