Version 1.7 Special Livestream Schedule: July 14 at 11:00 UTC

Published: July 9, 2026 · Official stream time: July 14, 2026 11:00 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble scheduled the Version 1.7 Special Livestream for July 14, 2026 at 11:00 UTC on the official The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin YouTube channel. The official preview lists Gowther skills and gameplay, a new main quest, and new content as the main highlights. The stream is planned to run simultaneously across Korean, Japanese, and Global channels, with a replay available after it ends.

Livestream schedule

  • Date and time: July 14, 2026 at 11:00 UTC.
  • Platform: official The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin YouTube channel.
  • Regions: Korean, Japanese, and Global channels are listed for simultaneous streaming.
  • Replay: the official notice says a replay will be available after the livestream ends.

Confirmed highlights

  • New character Gowther skills and gameplay.
  • New main quest preview.
  • New content preview for Version 1.7.

Cast listed by region

The official notice lists Korea hosts Sungkyun Oh and Hyo Jin Kim with guests MovingTV and Dongwolryang; Japan host Kousuke Hiraiwa, AMC Yuka Nihei, and guests Ryohei Kimura, Yuhei Takagi, Katapado, and Itatoha; and Global hosts Gclef and Jenn Lee.

What players should do before the stream

Treat this livestream as a preview, not a final patch note. If you are planning pulls or resources, wait for the stream and the later Version 1.7 update details before making decisions around Gowther, new content, or the next banner cycle.
