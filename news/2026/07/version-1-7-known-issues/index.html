Version 1.7 Known Issues Updated July 15: Armor Set, Slater, Steam, and Raid Missions

Published: July 15, 2026 · Updated: July 15, 2026 09:55 UTC · Official source: Version 1.7 Known Issues

Direct answer: Netmarble's July 15 Version 1.7 known-issues notice now lists eight active issues after the 09:55 UTC update. The newest entry is an Armor Set piece-count display issue; the actual Armor Set effect is still applied correctly based on equipped armor. Other practical issues include missing Slater after Act 15, Raid Missions - Conqueror's Path showing "Always Available," Steam error 30021225-0, party movement to the wrong area, and characters falling under the ground.

Active Version 1.7 issues

  • The number of equipped Armor Set pieces for the currently selected weapon type may not display correctly. Official note: the actual Armor Set effect is applied correctly based on the equipped armor.
  • The weapon type button may not appear in the upper right corner when selecting Quick Equip for an Accessory in the Bag.
  • The Top % text for your record may not appear in Leaderboard Previous Season Ranking Info.
  • NPC Slater may not appear when moving to Liones Castle after clearing Main Quest Act 15. Official temporary fix: reconnect to the game.
  • Raid Missions - Conqueror's Path may show the event period as Always Available on the mission screen. Official note: the missions are available until the August 26 maintenance.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 can block access. Official fix: update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to a specific area. Official temporary fix: reconnect to the game.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground. The official notice says this will be addressed in a future update.

Raid Missions date note

The July 15 known-issues notice says the Raid Missions event missions are available until the August 26 maintenance, even if the mission screen displays Always Available. This is a display issue, not a permanent-event confirmation.

What should players do now?

If you hit Slater visibility, party movement, or similar state issues, reconnect first because that is the official temporary fix for those entries. If Steam access fails with 30021225-0, update the Steam build before trying deeper troubleshooting.
