Confirmed Twigo Event: Tickets, Reward Board, and Fever Star Fragments

Published: July 22, 2026 · Event period: after July 22 maintenance until August 5 maintenance · Official event

Direct answer: Confirmed! Event - Confirmed! Twigo runs after the July 22 temporary maintenance until the August 5 maintenance. Complete Confirmed! Missions to earn up to Confirmed! Twigo Event Ticket x343, then spend tickets on the event board for Star Fragments, Radiant Potions of Charm, Tailor's Stitching Needles, Engraver's Fabric Scissors, Platinum Ingots, and crafting rewards.

Event requirement and ticket source

You must complete Main Quest HP Recovery!. Confirmed! Missions are split into seven phases, and clearing missions grants Confirmed! Twigo Event Tickets. The official maximum listed for completing all phases is 343 event tickets.

How the reward board works

The board has four rows with one, two, three, and four reward slots from top to bottom. Holding the Attack button consumes the number of tickets shown on the button and grants all rewards in that row. Rearrange randomly changes the rewards on the board after its cooldown.

Best rewards to notice

  • Star Fragment x300 can appear in Slot 4, with a listed maximum of 2.
  • Fever Time can grant Star Fragment x400 without consuming event tickets, up to 4 times during the event period.
  • Other notable rewards include Radiant Potions of Charm, Platinum Ingots, Tailor's Stitching Needles, Engraver's Fabric Scissors, Sunstone, Malachite, crafting kits, and blueprint selection chests.

Important event notes

Confirmed! Twigo Event Tickets only work during this event period. Unused tickets reset after the event ends, and rewards cannot be claimed after the event ends, so do not leave board rewards unclaimed.
