Gowther Pick Up and Exclusive Skin: 80 Draw Guarantee, 120th Draw Bonus, and Trial Rewards

Published: July 14, 2026 · Official source: Gowther Pick Up & Exclusive Skin

Direct answer: The Gowther Pick Up - A Doll Yearning for a Heart banner runs after the July 15 maintenance until the August 5 maintenance. The banner uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets, requires the main quest HP Recovery!, guarantees an SSR within 80 draws, gives a 50% chance for the pickup hero when an SSR appears, and guarantees the pickup hero on the next SSR if the previous SSR was not Gowther.

Pickup rules that matter

  • Event period: after July 15 maintenance to August 5 maintenance.
  • Unlock requirement: complete Main Quest HP Recovery!.
  • 80 draw guarantee: at least one SSR appears within 80 draws.
  • Pickup chance: when an SSR appears, the pickup hero has a 50% chance to appear.
  • Fail-safe: if the pickup hero does not appear from that SSR, the next SSR is guaranteed to be the pickup hero.
  • Carryover: accumulated draw count and guaranteed pickup status carry over to another Hero Pick Up banner.

120th Draw Bonus

The 120th Draw Bonus guarantees the pickup hero once on this banner. Official wording says this 120th bonus gauge does not reset even if you obtain the pickup hero before reaching 120 draws, and the 120th Draw Bonus does not carry over to other Hero Pick Up events.

Gowther Trial rewards

The Hero Gowther Trial Event runs during the same banner window. Listed rewards include Star Fragment x300, Supreme Mastery EXP x30, Unnamed Knight's Token x4, Bigmoss Young Flower x2, Vivid Wild Token x2, Rough Giant Bird Feather x2, and Mermaid's Tear x2.

Exclusive skin draw

A Journey to Find Myself is Gowther's exclusive skin draw. The first draw is free, Top Tier rewards start from the fifth draw, duplicate rewards do not appear, and all rewards including the outfit and weapon skin are guaranteed within 10 draws. Exclusive Skin Draw Tickets do not expire and can be used on future Exclusive Skin draw banners.

Quick pull read

This announcement confirms the banner format and guarantees, but it is not a full meta verdict. Players should wait for live testing of Gowther's Wand, Grimoire, and Staff routes before treating him as mandatory. The most concrete reason to plan pulls now is the guarantee structure and the short July 15 to August 5 banner window.
