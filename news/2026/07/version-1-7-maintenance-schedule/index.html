Version 1.7 Maintenance Completed: Gowther Update Is Live

Published: July 14, 2026 · Updated: July 15, 2026 · Official source: "An Unknown Threat" Version 1.7 Maintenance Schedule [Completed]

Direct answer: Version 1.7 maintenance has been completed, and the game is available again. The official notice now marks the maintenance as completed after the July 15 update window. Gowther, Gowther's exclusive skin, Main Quest Act 15, Liones Northern Front, Durak Boss Challenge, Deep Spider Nest Raid, Ranking Season 2, and Version 1.7 events are now live.

Maintenance result

  • Status: completed.
  • Maintenance window: July 15, 2026, 02:00-09:00 UTC.
  • Maintenance compensation: Star Fragment x300.
  • Compensation claim deadline: July 29, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.
  • Survey reward: Star Fragment x300 for eligible 100-Day Anniversary Survey submissions, sent during maintenance and available in the mailbox for 30 days.

New content now live

The completed update adds Gowther, Gowther's exclusive skin, Main Quest Act 15, Liones Northern Front, the Durak Boss Challenge, Deep Spider Nest Raid, Ranking Season 2, Hero Gowther Trial, A Journey to Find a Heart check-in, and Raid Missions - Conqueror's Path.

What changed for banner routing?

After the completed maintenance, Gowther should be treated as the active Version 1.7 pickup banner. Elizabeth and the Version 1.6 event tracks have moved out of the current-route position.

Known issues after maintenance

Netmarble also published a new Version 1.7 known-issues notice after maintenance, including Slater visibility after Act 15, Raid Missions event-period display, Steam error 30021225-0, party movement, and falling under the ground.
