Version 1.7 Maintenance Schedule: July 15 02:00-09:00 UTC

Published: July 14, 2026 · Official source: "An Unknown Threat" Version 1.7 Maintenance Schedule

Direct answer: Version 1.7 maintenance is scheduled for July 15, 2026 from 02:00 to 09:00 UTC. Netmarble lists Star Fragment x300 as maintenance compensation, plus a separate Star Fragment x300 survey reward distribution for eligible 100-Day survey participants.

Maintenance checklist

  • Downtime: July 15, 2026, 02:00-09:00 UTC.
  • Maintenance compensation: Star Fragment x300.
  • Compensation claim deadline: July 29, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.
  • Survey reward: Star Fragment x300 for eligible 100-Day Anniversary Survey submissions, sent during maintenance and available in the mailbox for 30 days.
  • Mystic Keys: the notice says only used keys are replenished during the reset, so use current Mystic Keys before maintenance if you want them restored.

New content after maintenance

The maintenance adds Gowther, Gowther's exclusive skin, Main Quest Act 15, Liones Northern Front, the Durak Boss Challenge, Deep Spider Nest Raid, Hero Gowther Trial, A Journey to Find a Heart check-in, and Raid Missions - Conqueror's Path.

Ending before Version 1.7

The notice lists multiple ending tracks at July 15 maintenance: Elizabeth Pick Up and skin content, Vision of the Azure Waves, Hero Elizabeth Trial, 100-Day Celebration Check-In, A Midsummer Day's Dream, Gotcha Scurry Squad, Exciting Cube Unboxing, Magic Pop, The Crimson Boar Appears, and Ranking Season 1.

Player priority before downtime

Use any current Mystic Keys you want replenished, finish event claims for Magic Pop and Exciting Cube Unboxing, and make final Elizabeth banner decisions before the maintenance window begins. After maintenance, the site should treat Gowther as the active Version 1.7 pickup.
