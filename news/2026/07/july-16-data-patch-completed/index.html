July 16 Data Patch Completed: Catapult and Deep Spider Nest

Published: July 16, 2026 · Completed: July 16, 2026 16:18 UTC · Official data patch

Direct answer: Netmarble says the July 16 data patch was completed at 16:18 UTC. Fully close and reconnect to download the new data. The patch adjusts Catapult behavior, removes Gold from the new Elite Monster Werewolf Chieftain reward list, and lowers normal enemy HP in the Deep Spider Nest raid.

Patch schedule

The official patch window was July 16, 2026 from 16:00 to 16:18 UTC. Players could be temporarily disconnected during the patch and should be able to play normally after reconnecting.

Patch details

  • Catapult enemies are defeated after being hit 1 time instead of 2 times.
  • The Catapult damage range was increased.
  • The battlefield seize requirement was reduced from 70% to 50%.
  • Allies take less damage from the Catapult.
  • Enemy HP no longer increases during party play for the Catapult.
  • Gold was removed from the reward list of the new Elite Monster Werewolf Chieftain.
  • Normal enemy HP was decreased in the Deep Spider Nest raid.

What should players do?

Completely close the game and reconnect before judging whether the Catapult, Werewolf Chieftain, or Deep Spider Nest changes are live on your account. If something still looks wrong after reconnecting, compare it with the current Version 1.7 known-issues list.
