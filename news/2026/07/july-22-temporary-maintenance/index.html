July 22 Temporary Maintenance Completed: Knighthood Boss Battle, Sector 9, and Fixes

Published: July 21, 2026 · Completed: July 22, 2026 08:00 UTC · Official notice

Direct answer: Netmarble marked the July 22 temporary maintenance as completed at 08:00 UTC. The update adds Knighthood Boss Battle content, Timespace Junction Sector 9, the Confirmed! Twigo event, and several Version 1.7 bug fixes. Compensation is Star Fragment x300.

Maintenance schedule and compensation

The official maintenance window was July 22, 2026 from 05:00 to 08:00 UTC. Star Fragment x300 can be claimed from the in-game mailbox after maintenance until August 5, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

New content in this maintenance

  • Knighthood Boss Battle.
  • Knighthood Combat Class and Boss Battle rankings.
  • Knighthood Shop.
  • Timespace Junction Sector 9.
  • Confirmed! Event - Confirmed! Twigo.

Bug fixes and ranking reset

The maintenance is scheduled to fix the Durak Boss Challenge retry field-reset issue. Netmarble says the overall rankings for Boss Challenge [Abyss] 「Corrupted」 Barbarian Chieftain Durak will be reset, and players who already had a record before the reset will receive [Honor] Laurel of Glory through the mailbox based on their ranking status at the start of maintenance.

Other official fixes

  • Armor Set piece counts for the selected weapon type not displaying correctly.
  • NPC Slater not appearing when moving to Liones Castle after clearing Main Quest Act 15.
  • Incorrect message when equipping a weapon already equipped to another hero.
  • Ranged attack damage not applying if a tag switch occurs after the attack but before it hits.
  • Unnatural enemy movement during Breaking Through.
  • Entry into the Barbarian Outpost region through the sand dome before the region officially opened.
  • Party members returning to individual Main Quest locations after disbanding a party after matchmaking ends.
  • Raid Missions - Conqueror's Path event period displaying as Always Available on the mission screen.
