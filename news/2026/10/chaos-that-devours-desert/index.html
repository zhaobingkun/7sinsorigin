Chaos That Devours the Desert Event

Published October 14, 2026 · official notice time 07:01 UTC

Direct answer: The new Chaos That Devours the Desert Hunting Event runs from October 14 at 07:00 through November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC. Complete the event quest, unlock the Kalbash Hunt, use up to two daily Desert Keeper’s Keys, and spend Golden Sand Tokens before the event ends.

How do I unlock the event?

  • Complete Main Quest Act 12 - Episode 15, “Corrupted” Ancient Dragon.
  • Event quests unlock sequentially from Day 1 through Day 5 through NPC Ensia.
  • Clearing Beings of Deep Darkness in the event quest opens the Hunt.

What are the Kalbash dungeon rules?

  • The Hermit Beneath the Sand follows Hunting Event rules and supports 10–15 players.
  • It is available only at Abyss difficulty; Party Matchmaking supports solo entry or parties of up to five.
  • The first clear gives a Lichdragon’s Roar Weapon Selection Chest.
  • Up to two Desert Keeper’s Keys are provided daily and reset at 07:00 UTC.

What rewards are confirmed?

Completing all daily-unlocked quests gives up to Star Fragment ×1,000, one Lichdragon’s Roar Weapon Selection Chest, Golden Sand Token ×550, and Gold ×100,000. Completing all event missions gives Desert Keeper’s Key ×5, Large Cube Key Bundle ×15, and Element Spell Bead Selection Chest ×25. Completing all seasonal missions gives Golden Sand Token ×500.

What is in the exchange shop?

Golden Sand Tokens can be exchanged for the Desert Lullaby title, a Lichdragon’s Roar Weapon Selection Chest, Oasis Whisper earrings, necklace and ring, SSR Magic Crystal Shards, Transcendence Engraving and Refinement Stones, Platinum Ingots, Special Mastery EXP, enhancement and refinement stones, Kingdom of Liones Seals, and Gold. The official notice says exchange limits and token costs are shown in-game.

Unused Golden Sand Tokens reset after the event, and rewards cannot be claimed after the event ends.

Official source: Hunting Event - Chaos That Devours the Desert.
