Version 2.2 Known Issues Updated October 13

Published October 8, 2026 · official notice updated October 13 at 10:30 UTC

Direct answer: The official Version 2.2 issue notice now also lists a Draco King Drake loot-quality problem and an iOS controller navigation problem. The current list includes Timespace Junction: Extra and Draw Bingo, incorrect Hawk Pass XP, Steam access error 30021225-0, party-leave movement, and characters falling beneath the ground. Netmarble marks the Knighthood Boss Battle reset issue, Knighthood Headquarters entry issue, and Act 17 tutorial-popup progression issue as resolved.

What issues are currently listed?

  • In Draco King Drake Boss Challenge, a higher difficulty may yield a lower Quality “Tyrant Dragon's Black Heart Top” than a lower difficulty.
  • On iOS with a controller connected, the Timespace Junction: Extra tab in the Version 2.2 Update Overview may not be selectable.
  • When a controller is connected, the cursor may intermittently fail to appear after entering Events → Draw Bingo.
  • Hawk Pass Mission rewards may apply the wrong amount of Hawk Pass XP.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; updating the Steam build to the latest version is the official workaround.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area; reconnecting can temporarily resolve it.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; the notice says this will be addressed in a future update.

Which problems were resolved?

The notice marks the Knighthood Boss Battle attempts not resetting after the October 13 maintenance as resolved on October 13. It also marks intermittent inability to enter Knighthood Headquarters and the Act 17 “Leave It to Me!” tutorial popup that blocked progression as resolved on October 8. A separate October 8 server patch fixed both issues and added a Ferzen Mines Raid Tag Skill fix.

What should players do?

Fully close and reconnect after applying any platform or Steam update. If a listed issue remains, use the workaround above and check the official notice again; do not treat the unresolved items as fixed until Netmarble updates the source.
