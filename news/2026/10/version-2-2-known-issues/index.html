Version 2.2 Known Issues Updated October 19

Published October 8, 2026 · official notice updated October 19 at 02:05 UTC

Direct answer: The official Version 2.2 issue notice updated October 19 adds a Chaos event mission-counting problem: obtaining SSR Equipment from the Draco King Drake Boss Challenge may not count toward Event Mission V, “Obtain SSR Equipment in Boss Challenge.” The current list also includes PS5 party entry, Draco King Drake loot quality, iOS controller navigation, Draw Bingo, incorrect Hawk Pass XP, Steam access error 30021225-0, party-leave movement, and characters falling beneath the ground.

What issues are currently listed?

  • In Chaos That Devours the Desert, obtaining SSR Equipment from the Draco King Drake Boss Challenge may not count for Event Mission V, “Obtain SSR Equipment in Boss Challenge.”
  • On PlayStation®5, [Party Introduction] → [Enter Manually] may not open when “Set to Searchable” is active.
  • In Draco King Drake Boss Challenge, a higher difficulty may yield a lower Quality “Tyrant Dragon's Black Heart Top” than a lower difficulty.
  • On iOS with a controller connected, the Timespace Junction: Extra tab in the Version 2.2 Update Overview may not be selectable.
  • When a controller is connected, the cursor may intermittently fail to appear after entering Events → Draw Bingo.
  • Hawk Pass Mission rewards may apply the wrong amount of Hawk Pass XP.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; updating the Steam build to the latest version is the official workaround.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area; reconnecting can temporarily resolve it.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; the notice says this will be addressed in a future update.

Which problems were resolved?

The notice marks Merlin’s Wand Special Attack “Electromagnetic Field” animation interruption on mobile as resolved October 8, the Knighthood Boss Battle attempts not resetting after the October 13 maintenance as resolved October 13, and intermittent inability to enter Knighthood Headquarters plus the Act 17 “Leave It to Me!” tutorial popup as resolved October 8. A separate October 8 server patch also fixed a Ferzen Mines Raid Tag Skill issue.

What should players do?

Fully close and reconnect after applying any platform or Steam update. For the Chaos event mission, check the mission state after obtaining SSR Equipment and keep the official notice bookmarked; Netmarble has not published a workaround for the counter issue. Do not treat the unresolved items as fixed until Netmarble updates the source.

Official source: Version 2.2 Known Issues (updated October 19 at 02:05 UTC).
