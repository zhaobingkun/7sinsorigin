Version 2.3 Issues and October 30 Data Patch

Official issue notice published October 28, 2026 · issue list updated October 29 · data patch completed October 30 at 08:15 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's October 30 data patch fixed the intermittent Mighty Freaky Circus event-map entrance problem after leaving the maze. It also improved chess-piece targeting detection and significantly reduced chess-piece HP in Taranis's Sanctuary. The separate Version 2.3 issue notice still lists Weekly Reward recharge-screen, Estia Growth Mission Bundle label, locked Timespace ranking-label, Steam, party-movement and falling-beneath-ground issues.

Which Version 2.3 problems are still listed?

  • The October 30 data patch fixed the Mighty Freaky Circus maze-exit entrance issue. If the patch is not applied, fully close the game and reconnect to download it.
  • The Weekly Rewards section may show the [Recharge] screen incorrectly after the maximum weekly Raid reward claims are reached.
  • Estia Growth Mission Bundle I–II purchase popups may show an incorrect “Event” label on reward items.
  • The main screen of Timespace Junction Season 2 may show a [Ranked] label below sectors whose rankings are not unlocked yet.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access to the game.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground.

What official workarounds are available?

For Steam error 30021225-0, Netmarble says to update the Steam build to the latest version. Reconnecting can temporarily resolve the party-movement issue. For the maze entrance fix, fully close and reconnect so the October 30 data patch downloads. The notice does not publish a workaround for the reward-screen, label or falling-beneath-ground issues.

What changed in the October 28 data patch?

The related data patch added a Leave button below the minimap while navigating the Mighty Freaky Circus maze. If you leave and re-enter, the game resumes from the starting point of the maze you were exploring. This is a navigation improvement, separate from the October 30 entrance-interaction fix.

What did the October 30 data patch change in the Raid?

Netmarble improved targeting detection for chess pieces in Taranis's Sanctuary and significantly reduced their HP. These are official data-patch changes, not a universal clear-time or team-strength conclusion; inspect the live raid behavior before changing a build.
