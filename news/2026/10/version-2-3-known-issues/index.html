Version 2.3 Known Issues Updated October 29

Official notice published October 28, 2026 · updated October 29 at 05:28 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's Version 2.3 issue notice lists a new problem where the Mighty Freaky Circus event-map entrance may intermittently stop responding after leaving the maze. The notice says this will be resolved during the November 4 maintenance. It also lists Weekly Reward recharge-screen, Estia Growth Mission Bundle label, locked Timespace ranking-label, Steam, party-movement and falling-beneath-ground issues.

Which Version 2.3 problems are still listed?

  • Mighty Freaky Circus maze: after leaving the maze during an event quest, the event-map entrance may intermittently become unusable in certain situations. Netmarble says this will be resolved during the November 4 maintenance.
  • The Weekly Rewards section may show the [Recharge] screen incorrectly after the maximum weekly Raid reward claims are reached.
  • Estia Growth Mission Bundle I–II purchase popups may show an incorrect “Event” label on reward items.
  • The main screen of Timespace Junction Season 2 may show a [Ranked] label below sectors whose rankings are not unlocked yet.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access to the game.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground.

What official workarounds are available?

For Steam error 30021225-0, Netmarble says to update the Steam build to the latest version. Reconnecting can temporarily resolve the party-movement issue. The notice does not publish a workaround for the reward-screen, label or falling-beneath-ground issues. Do not treat a planned maintenance fix as already deployed.

What changed in the October 28 data patch?

The related data patch added a Leave button below the minimap while navigating the Mighty Freaky Circus maze. If you leave and re-enter, the game resumes from the starting point of the maze you were exploring. This is a navigation improvement, separate from the later-listed entrance-interaction issue.

Official source: Version 2.3 Known Issues · October 28 Data Patch.
