Version 2.3 Known Issues Updated November 6

Official issue notice published October 28, 2026 · issue list updated November 6 at 09:43 UTC · data patch completed October 30 at 08:15 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's November 6 update adds two Demonic Beast Akumu combat issues and a post-Massive Corruption Frenzy graphics/swimming issue. It also keeps the Cheeeeeese Escape quest error, Weekly Reward recharge-screen issue, Estia bundle label issue, Timespace ranking-label issue, Steam access error, party-movement issue and falling-beneath-ground issue listed. The separate Swiftest Showdown Gold issue was scheduled for a fix during the completed November 5 maintenance, with missing Gold to be distributed sequentially; the notice does not publish a separate resolved marker.

Which Version 2.3 problems are still listed?

  • During the Knighthood Boss Battle, players may sometimes take no damage from certain Demonic Beast Akumu attack patterns. Netmarble added this on November 6 and scheduled a patch after the November 9 boss battle.
  • Demonic Beast Akumu's Defense buff may be removed in certain situations during the Knighthood Boss Battle. Netmarble also scheduled this fix after the November 9 boss battle.
  • After participating in Massive Corruption Frenzy, hero graphics may intermittently fail to display while gliding and players may be unable to swim. Reconnecting is the official temporary workaround.
  • The ‘The Cheeeeeese Escape’ quest in [Event Story - Mighty Freaky Circus] may intermittently show an error that prevents progress or exiting the quest. Netmarble added this issue on November 2 and did not publish a workaround in the notice.
  • The October 30 data patch fixed the separate Mighty Freaky Circus maze-exit entrance issue. If the patch is not applied, fully close the game and reconnect to download it.
  • The Weekly Rewards section may show the [Recharge] screen incorrectly after the maximum weekly Raid reward claims are reached.
  • Estia Growth Mission Bundle I–II purchase popups may show an incorrect “Event” label on reward items.
  • The main screen of Timespace Junction Season 2 may show a [Ranked] label below sectors whose rankings are not unlocked yet.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access to the game.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground.

What official workarounds are available?

For Steam error 30021225-0, Netmarble says to update the Steam build to the latest version. Reconnecting can temporarily resolve the party-movement issue and the post-Massive Corruption Frenzy gliding/swimming issue. For the maze entrance fix, fully close and reconnect so the October 30 data patch downloads. The two Akumu issues are scheduled for a patch after the November 9 boss battle, and the notice gives no interim workaround. The Swiftest Showdown notice says missing Gold will be distributed sequentially after the maintenance.

What changed in the October 28 data patch?

The related data patch added a Leave button below the minimap while navigating the Mighty Freaky Circus maze. If you leave and re-enter, the game resumes from the starting point of the maze you were exploring. This is a navigation improvement, separate from the October 30 entrance-interaction fix.

What did the October 30 data patch change in the Raid?

Netmarble improved targeting detection for chess pieces in Taranis's Sanctuary and significantly reduced their HP. These are official data-patch changes, not a universal clear-time or team-strength conclusion; inspect the live raid behavior before changing a build.
