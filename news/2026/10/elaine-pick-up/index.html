Elaine Pick Up: Whispers of the Wind

Elaine’s Hero Pick Up runs from after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance, UTC. The corrected event name is Whispers of the Wind; its unused 120th Draw Bonus awards Elaine at 120 draws on this banner.

First published: October 7, 2026 · Content corrected: October 11, 2026

Why does the official headline use a different name?

Official notice 328 retains Whisper of the Autumn Gale in its article title. Its October 8, 04:58 UTC correction changes the event name in the body and the banner image to Whispers of the Wind. These are the old and corrected names of the same Elaine event, not separate banners. This page follows the corrected body.

When is Elaine available, and which tickets work?

The announced period is after October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance, UTC, with an increased [SSR] Elaine rate. Complete the main quest HP Recovery! and use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets. Regular Hero tickets and Lancelot’s Exclusive Skin tickets are not the resources named for this event. The announcement does not provide an exact closing hour, so consult the maintenance notice before waiting until the final day.

What guarantees are written in Elaine’s own notice?

  • SSR pity: an SSR within 80 draws; an SSR obtained through ordinary draws resets the SSR guarantee count, even if it is not Elaine.
  • Conditional featured chance: when no featured guarantee is active, an SSR has a 50% chance to be the Pick Up hero. It is not a 50% chance on every individual draw.
  • After an off-featured SSR: the next SSR obtained is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.
  • Hero Pick Up carryover: the accumulated draw count and guaranteed Pick Up status carry to another Hero Pick Up.
  • 120th Draw Bonus: reaching 120 draws on this Elaine banner awards its featured hero as a bonus, once per banner. An early Elaine does not reset the gauge.
  • Bonus expiry: the bonus is valid only during its corresponding event period, and the gauge does not carry to another Hero Pick Up.

Full obtainable-hero and detailed probability information is in the bottom-left Rate Info panel. Notice 328 does not state a complete base SSR probability or soft-pity curve. It also does not explain whether the bonus award changes ordinary SSR pity or the featured-guarantee flag. Read the displayed state after claiming it before planning a later copy.

How does the bonus change an Elaine budget?

Starting fresh: with zero pity, no featured guarantee, and zero Elaine bonus progress, staying on Elaine’s banner guarantees a copy by its 120-draw bonus. Simply adding two 80-draw SSR cycles overstates the first-copy requirement when this bonus is available.

Entering with progress: with 60 carried SSR pity and an active featured guarantee, the next ordinary SSR is Elaine within at most 20 draws. With the same 60 pity but no featured guarantee and an unused fresh bonus gauge, the worst-case ordinary path is 20 draws to an off-featured SSR plus up to 80 more to Elaine, or 100 additional draws. The 120-draw bonus would be later, so 100 is the smaller bound.

Near the bonus: if Elaine’s bonus gauge is 110 and unclaimed, 10 further draws on Elaine trigger the bonus even if ordinary SSR pity is low. If you move to Lancelot, those 110 bonus draws do not transfer. If you already own Elaine, finishing those 10 draws is an extra-copy decision, not a requirement to keep ordinary pity.

These are deductions from the announced guarantees, not measured averages or account tests. See the pull-or-save budget table for how to combine the two routes and choose a stopping point.

How does this differ from Lancelot’s notice?

The full notices were checked independently on October 11. Their hero-ticket type, entry quest, event window, 80-draw SSR rule, conditional 50% featured rule, off-featured guarantee, carryover, and 120th Draw Bonus agree. Lancelot’s notice 327 additionally announces a Lancelot trial and a separate exclusive skin draw. Elaine’s notice 328 does not announce equivalent Elaine activities; those Lancelot sections cannot be generalized to her.

The same acquisition rules do not establish the same account value. This notice supplies no controlled performance comparison, named tier placement, or required duplicate count. Check Elaine’s in-game skills and your available teammates and upgrade materials before prioritizing her over another hero. The Elaine guide is a separate character reference.
