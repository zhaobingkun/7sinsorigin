Elaine Pick Up – Whisper of the Autumn Gale

Official banner notice: after October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance, UTC

Direct answer: Elaine Pick Up – Whisper of the Autumn Gale is the confirmed Elaine banner after the October 8 maintenance and runs until the October 28 maintenance in UTC.

What are the official draw rules?

  • Use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.
  • An SSR hero is guaranteed within 80 draws, with a 50% chance for the Pick Up hero.
  • If the Pick Up hero does not appear, the next SSR hero is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.

What should players avoid assuming?

The official notice confirms the banner period and standard Pick Up rules. It does not establish a universal tier ranking or whether Elaine is the best choice for every account; compare your roster and goals before spending.
