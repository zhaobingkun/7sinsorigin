Lancelot Pick Up – Heart-Piercing Eyes

Official banner notice: after October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance, UTC

Direct answer: Lancelot Pick Up – Heart-Piercing Eyes is the confirmed Version 2.2 banner after the October 8 maintenance and runs until the October 28 maintenance in UTC.

What are the official draw rules?

  • Use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.
  • An SSR hero is guaranteed within 80 draws, with a 50% chance for the Pick Up hero.
  • If the Pick Up hero does not appear, the next SSR hero is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.

What skin is connected to Lancelot?

The update also adds the Guardian's Resolve Outfit Skin and the Night Bleed Weapon Skin through the separate For What We Hold Dear draw. The first skin draw is free and all rewards are guaranteed within 10 draws.
