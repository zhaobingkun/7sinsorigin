Lancelot Pick Up: Heart-Piercing Eyes

Lancelot’s Heart-Piercing Eyes Hero Pick Up runs from after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance, UTC. An unused 120th Draw Bonus guarantees a Lancelot bonus at 120 draws on this banner, separately from SSR pity.

First published: October 7, 2026 · Content corrected: October 11, 2026

When can you draw, and what do you need?

The official Lancelot Pick Up & Exclusive Skin notice, id 327 confirms an increased [SSR] Lancelot rate during this window. Complete the main quest HP Recovery! to access the event and use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets for Heart-Piercing Eyes. The ending is tied to October 28 maintenance; this notice does not state a precise closing hour.

How do Lancelot’s guarantees work?

  • SSR guarantee: an SSR within 80 draws. Any SSR from ordinary draws resets this count, including a non-featured SSR.
  • Featured chance: without an active featured guarantee, an SSR has a 50% chance to be the Pick Up hero. This is conditional on an SSR, not a per-draw 50% rate.
  • After an off-featured SSR: the next SSR obtained is guaranteed to be the Pick Up hero.
  • Carryover: the accumulated Hero Pick Up draw count and guaranteed Pick Up status carry to another Hero Pick Up.
  • 120th Draw Bonus: reaching 120 draws on this banner awards Lancelot as a bonus, once per banner. Obtaining him early does not reset the bonus gauge.
  • Bonus boundary: this bonus is valid only during the corresponding banner period and does not carry to another Hero Pick Up.

For the full hero pool and detailed probabilities, open Rate Info at the bottom-left of the event screen. The notice does not explain whether the bonus award changes ordinary SSR pity or the featured-guarantee flag; read your displayed state after claiming it.

Budget example, derived from the rules: from zero SSR pity, no featured guarantee, and zero bonus progress, staying on Lancelot’s banner ensures a copy by 120 draws through the bonus. If you miss the featured hero at draw 80, ordinary SSR pity resets, but the bonus is now just 40 draws away. With a carried featured guarantee and 60 SSR pity, an ordinary featured SSR is instead within 20 draws. These are bounds, not average costs or observed account results.

What can the Lancelot trial tell you?

The trial runs during the same October 8–28 maintenance window. It lets you try [SSR] Lancelot and awards Star Fragment ×300, Supreme Mastery EXP ×30, Rough Giant Bird Feather ×4, Unnamed Knight’s Token ×4, Vivid Wild Token ×2, and Mermaid’s Tear ×2 for completing the challenge.

The supplied trial hero is fully upgraded and equipped only with Outfit and Weapon Skins. Use the trial to understand controls and skill flow; do not assume its damage is what an unbuilt copy will deliver on your account. The notice does not establish a comparative tier ranking. Read the official Lancelot skill summary before selecting a target.

How is the exclusive skin draw different?

For What We Hold Dear is a separate event during the same window, also requiring HP Recovery! It offers the Guardian’s Resolve Outfit Skin for [SSR] Lancelot and the Night Bleed Weapon Skin for his Staff. Skins change appearance without affecting stats, skills, or other attributes; obtaining a skin does not grant the hero.

The first draw is free; later draws use Exclusive Skin Draw Tickets, which do not expire after the event. Each draw grants one nonduplicate reward. Top Tier Rewards can begin at draw 5, and all rewards are guaranteed within 10 draws. Required tickets and probabilities vary by attempt.
