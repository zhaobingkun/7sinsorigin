Version 2.3 Enchantment and Engraved Equipment Bonus Locks

Published October 19, 2026 · official notice time 08:30 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble says the Version 2.3 update on October 28, 2026 will add a Bonus Lock feature for rerolling Enchantment and Engraved Equipment Bonuses. Players will be able to choose which bonuses stay locked while the other bonuses are rerolled.

When does the bonus-lock feature start?

The official notice schedules the change for the October 28 maintenance, when Version 2.3 is introduced. It is an announced upcoming change, not a feature that this notice says is already live.

How many bonuses can be locked?

  • For Engraved Equipment, up to two bonuses can be locked.
  • For Enchantment, any number of bonuses can be locked as long as at least one bonus remains unlocked for rerolling.

These limits are the official announcement's limits. The notice does not provide a worked example for a particular equipment piece or stat combination.

Does locking add another resource cost?

No additional resources will be required for the Bonus Lock feature, and Netmarble says the reroll cost will remain unchanged. The announcement does not give new values for existing materials, bonus rates, or final in-game interface wording.

What remains unconfirmed?

This notice confirms the lock mechanic and schedule, but it does not establish a new equipment tier list, preferred stat combinations, or a broader Version 2.3 content list. Test the live feature after maintenance before treating a lock pattern as a best build.

Official source: Regarding Changes to Enchantment and Engraved Equipment Bonuses.
