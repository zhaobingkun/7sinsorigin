Hero [SSR] Lancelot Details

Official release-version notice: October 7, 2026

Direct answer: Lancelot is an SSR hero who uses Staff, Longsword, or Gauntlets. The official notice lists Holy, Physical, and other weapon-specific skill kits, plus Fox Form as his Adventure Skill.

What is confirmed?

  • Fox Form gives capture attempts a 30% Lucky Boost chance, amplifying Capture Rate by 50% against the base rate.
  • The Staff kit is a Holy attacker built around Burst activation, Divine Rush, Burst Spear, and Swift Light.
  • The Longsword kit is a Physical attacker built around perfect Iai Stance charges and converted skills.
  • The notice also provides Gauntlets skills and ten Potential tiers.

How should this page be used?

These are official release-version descriptions, not a universal tier ranking. Netmarble warns that details may change through future patches; use the in-game descriptions for the most current values.
