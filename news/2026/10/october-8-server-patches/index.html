October 8 Server Patches Fix Act 17 and Raid Issues

Published and completed: October 8, 2026 · final patch window: 12:37–13:57 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble published two October 8 server-patch notices. The earlier 10:59 UTC patch fixed an Act 17 tutorial popup that could block progression; the later 12:37–13:57 UTC patch fixed intermittent Knighthood Headquarters entry and a Ferzen Mines Raid Tag Skill issue.

What did the October 8 patches fix?

  • The Act 17 “Leave It to Me!” tutorial popup no longer blocks players from progressing.
  • Players should no longer be intermittently unable to enter Knighthood Headquarters.
  • In the Ferzen Mines Raid against “Abyssal” Predator Long-dead, team heroes’ Tag Skills can be used after neutralizing the boss.

When were the patches applied?

The first notice scheduled a sequential patch at 10:59 UTC. The completion notice records the later patch as 12:37–13:57 UTC and says the data is applied after fully closing and reconnecting to the game.

What should players do?

Completely close the game and reconnect to download the new data. Temporary disconnections can occur while the sequential patch is applied; retry the affected Act 17, Knighthood, or Raid content after reconnecting.

Does this change the current banners?

No. These notices are bug-fix announcements. They do not change the confirmed Lancelot and Elaine banner windows or confirm a successor after the October 28 maintenance.

Official sources: October 8 Server Patch and October 8 Server Patch [Completed].
