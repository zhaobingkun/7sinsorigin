Estia Official Details: Weapons, Cold Attribute, and Penguin Form

Official hero notice published October 27, 2026 · Version 2.3 preview

Direct answer: Netmarble confirms Estia as a new SSR hero for Version 2.3. The official details list Rapier, Wand and Grimoire weapon profiles, Cold-attribute roles, and the Adventure Skill Penguin Form!, which is faster in water and unaffected by underwater contamination.

What does the official Estia notice confirm?

  • Estia is an SSR hero with Rapier, Wand and Grimoire weapon profiles.
  • Her Rapier profile is a Cold Attacker, and her Grimoire profile is a Cold Support.
  • Penguin Form! changes her movement form, making her slow on land but faster than anyone in water.
  • She is featured in the post-October 28 Pick Up The Power to Neutralize Pollution.

What should players check in the game?

The notice says its information is based on the release version and may change through future patches. It also directs players to in-game descriptions for the most accurate skill details. Do not turn the published skill preview into a universal tier placement or damage comparison before testing the live hero and your own team.

How does this affect banner planning?

Use the current banner tracker for the announced Version 2.3 window and the full Version 2.3 update summary for event deadlines. The hero notice itself is not a substitute for the in-game Rate Info panel or a personal pull budget.
