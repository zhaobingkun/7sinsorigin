Draw Bingo Event

After October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance, UTC

Direct answer: Complete event missions to earn Bingo Tickets and Chance Coins, draw numbers, complete Bingo lines, and claim Star Fragments, materials, and weapon skins.

How do Bingo Tickets work?

  • Daily Missions can award up to 7 Bingo Tickets per day.
  • Chance Missions award up to 15 Chance Coins, while Call Missions award up to 36 Bingo Tickets.
  • Three Chance Coins can be exchanged for one Bingo number of your choice.
  • Daily missions reset at 07:00 UTC; unused event currency resets when the event ends.

What are the headline rewards?

Completing lines and boards can award Star Fragments, Brilliant Engraving Seals, Cube Keys, and the universal Zephyr's Echo and Luminous Scale weapon skins.

Official source: Draw Bingo.
