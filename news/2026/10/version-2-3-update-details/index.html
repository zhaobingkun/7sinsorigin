Version 2.3 Update Details: Estia, Clotho, and New Systems

Official notice published October 27, 2026 · updated October 28 at 01:50 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble's official Version 2.3 details confirm Estia and a returning Clotho Pick Up window after the October 28 maintenance through the November 18 maintenance. The update also adds Halloween events, Taranis's Sanctuary [Transcendence], Greed Incarnate equipment, Ranking Season 7, a new Memory of Dimensions quest, and World Level 5 systems that unlock after the November 4 maintenance.

Which Version 2.3 banners are confirmed?

Estia's Pick Up is The Power to Neutralize Pollution, and Clotho's rerun is Enigmatic Helper. Both are announced for after the October 28 maintenance until the November 18 maintenance, UTC, and use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets. The notice confirms increased featured-hero rates, but it does not replace each banner's own Rate Info panel for numeric rates or guarantee details. Use the current banner tracker and do not carry Version 2.2 assumptions into a new event without checking its notice.

What new content arrives?

  • Taranis's Sanctuary [Transcendence]: a higher raid difficulty using Mystic Keys, with no separate time limit or Pledges at launch.
  • Greed Incarnate: a fixed-Quality-170 higher-tier set, with Greedy Ruler also appearing from elite monsters and weekly chance chests.
  • Ranking Season 7: the Star of the Nightmare Transcendence challenge and Timespace Junction Season 2 Sectors 7 and 8.
  • Memory of Dimensions: the new quest The Holy Knight's Sword.
  • World Level 5 and Star Map: World Level 5 opens after the November 4 maintenance at Book of Stars Level 50, with Corruption Frenzy encounters and permanent Attack, Defense and HP progression.

What are the Halloween and November events?

Mighty Freaky Circus adds the Star of the Nightmare boss challenge and daily Circus Entry Tickets. Deadly Sins, Assemble! is a merge-style minigame with its own exchange rewards. The Update Eve Check-In and Penguin Smash begin after the November 4 maintenance, while Swiftest Showdown runs through the later part of the update window and offers up to 2,400 Star Fragments from its missions.

What should players check before the update?

Raid keys and weekly reward counts reset during the October 28 maintenance; only the number used before the update is replenished. Knighthood Boss Battle is temporarily unavailable after the November 2 weekly reset and reopens after the November 4 maintenance. The October 28 data patch also added a Leave button below the minimap in the Mighty Freaky Circus maze; leaving and re-entering resumes from the maze's starting point. Read the event deadline checklist before spending keys or leaving mailbox rewards unclaimed.

What remains unconfirmed?

This notice does not establish a universal tier ranking, comparative damage result, future Version 2.4 schedule, or a guarantee that every planned feature will arrive on a particular date. Banner strength and account value still require your roster, the in-game Rate Info panel and live testing.
