Version 2.2 Special Livestream: October 7 Schedule

Published: October 1, 2026 · Officially checked: October 1, 2026 10:00 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble scheduled the official Version 2.2 Special Livestream for October 7, 2026 at 11:00 UTC. The global broadcast will preview Lancelot skills and gameplay, a new main story, new content, and rewards.

When and where is the livestream?

The Korean, Japanese, and Global channels are scheduled to stream simultaneously. The official notice says the time may change, and a replay will be available after the broadcast ends.

Watch the official YouTube livestream

What will the official preview cover?

  • New character Lancelot skills and gameplay
  • A new main story
  • New content and related rewards

The Global stream lists Gclef and Lucy as MCs, with Sora and Kelbert as guests.

What is not confirmed yet?

This notice does not confirm Lancelot's banner, release date, role, draw rates, pity rules, or the final Version 2.2 maintenance window. Keep those details unconfirmed until Netmarble publishes them or shows them in the official livestream.
