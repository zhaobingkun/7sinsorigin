Version 2.2 Update Details

Official details published October 7, 2026 · updated October 14, 2026 at 09:25 UTC

Direct answer: Version 2.2 adds Lancelot, Elaine Pick Up, Act 17 and Cordin Village, Draco King Drake difficulties, Ranking Season 6, Timespace Extra Sectors 1–12, new recipes, the October Hunting Event, and additional fixes.

What is live after the update?

  • Lancelot Pick Up and Elaine Pick Up run after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance.
  • Act 17, “The Silent Village and the Guardian of Water,” adds Cordin Village.
  • Draco King Drake gains Hell, Abyss, and Transcendence difficulties.
  • Timespace Junction adds Normal, Ranked, and Extra paths; Extra Sectors 1–12 open after maintenance.
  • Ranking Season 6 gives participating players 10 Laurels of Glory regardless of rank.

What is the current Hunting Event?

Chaos That Devours the Desert runs from October 14 at 07:00 through November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC. It adds sequential event quests, the Kalbash dungeon, up to two daily Desert Keeper’s Keys, and Golden Sand Token rewards. See the full event guide for quest, mission, first-clear, and exchange-shop details.

Which official fixes matter?

The October 14 update marks Merlin’s Wand Special Attack “Electromagnetic Field” animation interruption on mobile as resolved. The related Version 2.2 issue notice also adds a PS5 party-introduction entry issue; see the current issue list before troubleshooting.

What system changes matter?

Daily Missions now use a random region and unlock Recruitment Clues. Seven Recruitment Clues let players choose one hero from the listed SSR pool. SR and SSR Engraved Equipment can be salvaged, and new Cordin Village recipes and equipment are added.
