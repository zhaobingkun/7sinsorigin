Version 2.2 Update Details

Official details published October 7, 2026 · updated October 8, 2026 at 00:49 UTC

Direct answer: Version 2.2 adds Lancelot, Elaine Pick Up, Act 17 and Cordin Village, Draco King Drake difficulties, Ranking Season 6, Timespace Extra Sectors 1–12, new recipes, and several event tracks.

What is live after maintenance?

  • Lancelot Pick Up and Elaine Pick Up run after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance.
  • Act 17, “The Silent Village and the Guardian of Water,” adds Cordin Village.
  • Draco King Drake gains Hell, Abyss, and Transcendence difficulties.
  • Timespace Junction adds Normal, Ranked, and Extra paths; Extra Sectors 1–12 open after maintenance.
  • Ranking Season 6 gives participating players 10 Laurels of Glory regardless of rank.

Which events should players check?

Lancelot Trial, Update Celebration Check-In, Draw Bingo, and Exciting Cube Unboxing run through the October 28 maintenance. Chaos That Devours the Desert begins October 14 at 07:00 UTC and runs until November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC.

What system changes matter?

Daily Missions now use a random region and unlock Recruitment Clues. Seven Recruitment Clues let players choose one hero from the listed SSR pool. SR and SSR Engraved Equipment can be salvaged, and new Cordin Village recipes and equipment are added.
