October 20 Server Patch: Timespace Sector 12 Title

Official notice published October 20, 2026 · updated at 10:15 UTC · patch completed at 10:14 UTC

Direct answer: The completed October 20 server patch adds the title Relentless Challenger to the first-clear reward for Sector 12 of Timespace Junction: Extra Season 1. Players who already claimed that first-clear reward will receive the new title during the October 28 maintenance.

What did the October 20 patch change?

  • Added Relentless Challenger to the Sector 12 first-clear reward for Timespace Junction: Extra Season 1.
  • Added server-stability improvements.
  • The patch was applied from 09:40 to 10:14 UTC; players should fully close and reconnect to download the latest data.

What if I already cleared Sector 12?

Netmarble says players who already claimed the Sector 12 first-clear reward will receive the new title during the October 28 maintenance. The notice does not say that the first-clear reward must be claimed again.

Is the patch still ongoing?

No. The official notice marks the patch completed at 10:14 UTC on October 20. Reconnect once so the client downloads the new data, and install the latest platform update if prompted.

Official source: October 20 (Tue) Server Patch [Completed].
