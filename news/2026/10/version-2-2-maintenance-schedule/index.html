Version 2.2 Maintenance Schedule

Official schedule published October 7, 2026 · checked at 02:00 UTC

Direct answer: Version 2.2 maintenance is scheduled for October 8, 2026 from 02:00 to 09:00 UTC. The game will be unavailable during the seven-hour window, and Netmarble says the schedule may change.

What compensation is confirmed?

  • Maintenance compensation: Star Fragment x300.
  • Leaderboard Regular Season 5 participation: Star Fragment x1,000.
  • Timespace Junction Sector 3 participation: Laurel of Glory x20.
  • Timespace Junction Sector 4 participation: Laurel of Glory x20.

The leaderboard apology rewards are for players who participated in the listed sectors before maintenance. They can be claimed from the in-game mailbox after maintenance until October 28 at 14:59 UTC.

What arrives with Version 2.2?

  • New Hero Lancelot and the exclusive Guardian's Resolve skin
  • Main Quest Act 17, “The Silent Village and the Guardian of Water,” plus Cordin Village
  • Elaine Pick Up – Whisper of the Autumn Gale
  • Draco King Drake Boss Challenge difficulties: Hell, Abyss, and Transcendence
  • Ranking Season 6 and Timespace Junction Extra Sectors 1–12
  • Lancelot Trial, Update Celebration Check-In, Draw Bingo, and Exciting Cube Unboxing events

What ends around maintenance?

The official notice lists Khala Pick Up, Selective Pick Up, the current Version 2.1 events, Ranking Season 5, several skins and products, and the related event content as ending around the October 8 maintenance. Check the in-game notices before spending or claiming time-limited rewards.

What is still not confirmed?

This schedule is not the full Version 2.2 update-details notice. Exact banner rules, rates, pity behavior, and detailed gameplay values should remain unconfirmed until Netmarble publishes the separate update details.
