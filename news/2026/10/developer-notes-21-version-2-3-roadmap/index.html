Developer Notes #21: Version 2.3 Roadmap

Published October 23, 2026 · official article modified October 23, 2026 09:30 UTC

Direct answer: Developer Notes #21 confirms that Version 2.3 will add Bonus Locks, World Level 5 content, the Star Map progression system, a second Memory of Dimensions story, and higher-difficulty Taranis Raid content. It also describes the Greed Incarnate equipment set and a Knighthood Boss Battle Season 2 revamp. The note does not turn its Version 2.4 or 2027 roadmap into current release content.

What does Version 2.3 confirm?

  • Bonus Locks: lock selected Enchantment or Engraved Equipment bonuses without extra materials; reroll costs remain unchanged.
  • World Level 5: unlock after reaching Book of Stars Level 50 and clearing its dungeon.
  • Corruption Frenzy: solo Frenzy can cycle every 8 hours with up to three bosses active; Grand Frenzy is a scheduled, up-to-15-player mode with daily reward limits.
  • Star Map: an account-wide system using Frenzy rewards; solo content improves primary stats and Grand Frenzy improves secondary stats.
  • Memory of Dimensions: a second original story following Gilthunder in Vanya.
  • Taranis [Transcendence]: a higher difficulty with no additional Pledges initially and no clear-time limit.
  • Greed Incarnate: a new raid set above Greedy Ruler, with fixed Quality 170 for the new equipment and up to two weekly chance chests.

What happens to Knighthood Boss Battle?

Netmarble says Season 2 will revamp Demonic Beast Akumu. The separate reset notice gives the immediate October 26 downtime and October 28 restart schedule; use the Raid and Knighthood reset notice for those dates.

What is planned later, not confirmed for Version 2.3?

The notes discuss a second Boss Challenge revamp and a new Cordin Underground Waterway Raid for Version 2.4, optimization work, possible pet duplicate growth, a future 15-player event, combat-system review, and a new continent with bosses for 2027. These are development plans, not a live schedule or banner announcement.

What is the Version 2.3 celebration gift?

The official gift lists Transcendence Refinement Stone ×30, Transcendence Engraving Stone ×10, Remnants of the Rift ×5, Engraver's Fabric Scissors ×3, and Gold ×3,000,000. It is sent to the in-game mailbox and can be claimed until November 17, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

Official source: Developer Notes #21.
