Melascula Official Details: Weapons, Darkness Support, and Soul Mechanics

Official hero notice published November 18, 2026 · updated November 20 at 08:32 UTC · source article 373

Direct answer: Netmarble confirms Melascula as a Version 2.4 SSR hero who can use Rapier, Lance, and Sword and Shield. Her published Rapier profile is a Darkness support kit built around Soul Eater and Gloom Spirit, but the notice warns that release details may change and directs players to the in-game descriptions.

What does the official notice confirm?

  • Melascula is an SSR hero with Rapier, Lance, and Sword and Shield weapon options.
  • Her Adventure Skill, Cocoon of Life, charges Life Energy when damaged monsters die and restores Max HP when used.
  • The Rapier profile is labeled Darkness (Support). Soul Eater reduces enemy Crit Resistance, while Gloom Spirit reduces Crit Defense and restores Magic Gauge.
  • When both effects are active, the published skills can apply Soul Rip, reduce Shatter Resistance and Defense, and increase damage taken through the Ultimate Move.

How does she relate to the current banner?

Melascula appears in Melascula Pick Up — Soul Glutton after the November 19 maintenance until the December 9 maintenance, UTC. The banner requires the main quest HP Recovery! and uses Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets. Read the current tracker and the pull-or-save guide for budget planning.

What remains unconfirmed?

The official notice says its information is based on the release version and may change through future patches. It is not a controlled damage test or a universal tier placement.
