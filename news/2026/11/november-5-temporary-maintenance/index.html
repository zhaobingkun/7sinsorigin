November 5 Temporary Maintenance: World Level 5 and Star Map

Official notice updated November 3, 2026 at 10:58 UTC

Direct answer: the Version 2.3 temporary maintenance is scheduled for November 5, 02:00–09:00 UTC. Netmarble rescheduled it from November 4; events and Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 7 that were originally tied to maintenance instead open on November 4 at 07:00 UTC.

What opens with the maintenance?

  • World Level 5 content, subject to the Book of Stars progression requirement.
  • The Star Map system.
  • Demonic Beast Akumu Knighthood Boss Battle revamp and a new season.
  • Listed fixes for Knighthood Boss Battle, Corrupted Ancient Dragon, the Personal Boat, Timespace labels, Estia Growth Mission Bundle labels, Tioreh's Fire Ejection, skins and PlayStation 5 UI issues.

What should players claim or note?

  • Maintenance compensation is Star Fragment ×300 and is claimable from the mailbox until November 19 at 14:59 UTC.
  • Chaos That Devours the Desert ends on November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC.
  • The Friday-to-Monday Massive Corruption Frenzy schedule is listed in the official notice; Gray Demon and Red Demon are temporarily excluded after this maintenance.

The maintenance notice is not a banner-rate or pity announcement. Estia and Clotho remain covered by their separate Pick Up notices and the live in-game Rate Info panel.

Official source: November 5 Temporary Maintenance.
