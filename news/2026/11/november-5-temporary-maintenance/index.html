November 5 Temporary Maintenance: World Level 5 and Star Map

Official notice updated November 6, 2026 at 02:25 UTC · maintenance completed

Direct answer: the Version 2.3 temporary maintenance ran on November 5, 02:00–09:00 UTC and is complete. Netmarble rescheduled it from November 4; events and Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 7 opened separately on November 4 at 07:00 UTC.

What did the maintenance add?

  • World Level 5 content, the Star Map system and the Demonic Beast Akumu Knighthood Boss Battle revamp/new season.
  • Gray Demon and Red Demon are included in Massive Corruption Frenzy after this maintenance; the earlier temporary exclusion note was replaced on November 6.
  • Fixes for Knighthood Boss Battle delays, Corrupted Ancient Dragon HP restoration from Estia's Tidal Grove, the Personal Boat, locked Timespace ranking labels, Estia Growth Mission Bundle labels, Tioreh's Fire Ejection, skins and PlayStation 5 UI issues.

What should players claim or note?

  • Maintenance compensation is Star Fragment ×300, claimable from the mailbox until November 19 at 14:59 UTC.
  • All Star Guardians also receive 3 Corruption Frenzy Activation Tickets, claimable until November 19 at 14:59 UTC; the tickets work only at World Level 5 and within the stated three-boss limit.
  • Chaos That Devours the Desert ended on November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC.

The maintenance notice is not a banner-rate or pity announcement. Estia and Clotho remain covered by their separate Pick Up notices and the live in-game Rate Info panel.

Official source: November 5 Temporary Maintenance.
