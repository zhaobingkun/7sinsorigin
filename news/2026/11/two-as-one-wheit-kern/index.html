Wheit & Kern Event Boss: Rewards and Dates

November 25, 2026 at 07:00 UTC to December 16 at 06:59:59 UTC · official article 384

Direct answer: Two as One! Wheit & Kern runs from November 25 at 07:00 UTC through December 16 at 06:59:59 UTC. Complete the event missions, defeat the paired Event Boss and use Stolen Coins to open reward cubes.

Who can enter the Event Boss?

The official requirement is completion of the Main Quest Calm Days. The challenge offers Normal, Nightmare, Abyss and Transcendence difficulties, with Cube Keys awarded for each first clear.

How do Stolen Coins work?

Up to two Stolen Coins are provided daily and reset at 07:00 UTC. Daily missions can award one Cube Key Bundle and one Stolen Coin; completing all Effort Missions awards Remnants of the Rift ×10.

What rewards are listed?

Reward Cubes can contain Star Fragments, Gold, Special Enhancement Stones and Special Refinement Stones. The official notice also lists a chance-based SSR Crafted Weapon Select Chest. It does not publish a drop rate, so this page does not infer one.

Rewards cannot be claimed after the event ends. Check the in-game event screen for the live exchange quantities and claim state.

Official source: Event Boss - Two as One! Wheit & Kern.
