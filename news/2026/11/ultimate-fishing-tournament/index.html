Ultimate Fishing Tournament: Dates and Rewards

November 25, 2026 at 07:00 UTC until the December 9 maintenance · official article 385 updated November 25 at 08:16 UTC

Direct answer: The Ultimate Fishing Tournament runs from November 25 at 07:00 UTC until the December 9 Version 2.5 maintenance. Complete missions, catch and submit event fish for Shiny Scales, then use the event shop and ranking rewards before the event closes.

Who can enter the fishing tournament?

The official requirement is completion of the Main Quest Calm Days. The event fishing spot includes 16 types of event fish from 2★ to 5★. Event fish cannot be salvaged or used for cooking.

What should players collect?

Submit eligible fish to NPC Hector for Shiny Scales. Cordin Handmade Bait, Legendary Fish Secret Bait and Rozena Crystal Bait have separate uses; Rozena Crystal Bait can be used to catch the gliding pet Rozena Wave. The exchange shop also lists Bloody Wasp, a Longsword weapon skin with no additional effects, plus materials and currency.

How do rankings and resets work?

Regular Fish Size and Legendary Fish Size rankings are calculated daily at 07:00 UTC, with final regular-fish rewards at the end of the event. Catching fish automatically enters the rankings; regular ranking submissions are counted through Hector. Daily missions reset at 07:00 UTC, and remaining Shiny Scales, bait and event fish are reset when the event ends.

The updated notice adds that identical displayed fish sizes are ordered using more precise measurements and catch times that are not shown in-game. The official issue list also reports an intermittent inability to use Set the Hook; no specific workaround is published.

Official source: The Ultimate Fishing Tournament.
