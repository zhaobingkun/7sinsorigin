Version 2.4 Update Details: Melascula, Cordin, and New Systems

Official notice published November 17, 2026 · updated November 19 at 08:47 UTC · source article 372

Direct answer: Version 2.4 is live after the completed November 19 maintenance. The official details add Main Quest Act 18 and the Cordin Reservoir region, Cordin Underground Waterway, SSR Melascula, Gowther Pick Up, Ranking Season 8, Extra Season 2 content and a Boss Challenge revamp.

What is new in Version 2.4?

  • Story and region: Act 18, The Truth Beneath the Water, continues the Cordin story in Cordin Reservoir.
  • Raid: Cordin Underground Waterway opens at Hell, Abyss and Transcendence difficulties after Act 17 Episode 75, with Doomshadow and Explosive Wings equipment sets.
  • Boss Challenges: Engraving, Growth, Ranking and Event Boss tabs replace the older organization; Albion Mutant and Corrupted Warden Orgot are removed and related achievements are completed automatically.
  • Progression and quality of life: Mystic Key standard replenishment rises from 2 to 3, Hero Trials become permanent, Raid Pledge status appears on the clear screen, and Power-Saving Mode is added.
  • Timespace: Extra Season 2 Sectors 1–12 unlock after the maintenance; normal/ranked Sector 9 opened November 25 and Sector 10 is scheduled for December 2 at 07:00 UTC.

Which Version 2.4 banners are confirmed?

Melascula Pick Up — Soul Glutton — and Gowther Pick Up — A Doll Yearning for a Heart — run after the November 19 maintenance until the December 9 maintenance, UTC. Both use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets and their notices confirm an SSR within 80 draws, a conditional 50% featured chance, a featured guarantee after an off-featured SSR, and a separate once-per-banner 120th Draw Bonus. Check the live Rate Info panel for the complete pool and probabilities.

Which events and rewards should players claim?

The 14-day Update Celebration Check-In runs until the December 30 maintenance. Each day lets you choose one of two rewards; completing all 14 days grants the unchosen rewards as a bonus. Conqueror’s Path runs until the December 9 maintenance and can award up to five Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets after all Raid Missions. Charge! Make Way for the Mighty Hawk uses Piggy Prints for event-shop rewards, while The Ultimate Fishing Tournament and Two as One! Wheit & Kern open on November 25 at 07:00 UTC.

Which official coupons are time-limited?

The update notice lists melasculaishere, newraidcordin, keepfaith and gowtherrerun. The Version 2.4 Special Livestream coupon rewards can be redeemed until December 8 at 14:59 UTC through the official coupon page or in-game settings, depending on device. Check the redemption screen before relying on a reward.

What should not be inferred?

Official skill previews and update systems do not establish a universal tier ranking, best team or damage comparison. Use the live hero descriptions, Rate Info and your own account goals. Future roadmap comments remain plans unless a later notice confirms them.
