America West and America East Server Merger

Official notice published November 23, 2026 · planned for the December 9 Version 2.5 maintenance · source article 383

Direct answer: America (West) and America (East) will connect to the same merged server during the December 9 Version 2.5 maintenance. Both names remain on the server-selection screen, and party matchmaking will be integrated between them.

What happens to existing characters?

Selecting either server continues to access the character stored there. If you have no character on the selected server, the first login leads to character creation; creating a new character does not delete the character on the other server. Characters from the two servers cannot be accessed simultaneously across multiple devices or platforms, and purchase records remain separate.

What happens to Knighthoods and rankings?

If duplicate Knighthood names exist, the one created later receives _1. The affected Knighthood receives 300,000 Gold for a name change, sent to its Captain. Knighthood Boss Battles pause after the December 7 weekly reset at 07:00 UTC and a new season begins after the December 9 maintenance. Previous leaderboard records from both America servers reset after the merger.

What should affected players check?

Before the maintenance, note which character and server you select first after the merger because externally purchased items may be delivered to the first character you log into. Keep any purchase records and Knighthood details. This notice is a schedule and account-access explanation; it does not change banner rates or confirm future hero strength.

Official source: Upcoming Server Merger Notice.
