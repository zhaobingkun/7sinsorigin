Version 2.4 Known Issues Updated November 26

Official issue notice published November 19, 2026 · issue list updated November 26 at 02:13 UTC · source article 381

Direct answer: The current official Version 2.4 issue notice adds an intermittent problem where players may be unable to use Set the Hook while fishing in The Ultimate Fishing Tournament. Steam error 30021225-0, a party-leave movement issue and characters falling beneath the ground also remain listed; the notice does not publish a workaround for the new fishing issue.

Which Version 2.4 issues are still listed?

  • During The Ultimate Fishing Tournament, players may intermittently be unable to use Set the Hook; Netmarble says it was added on November 26 and gives no specific workaround.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may prevent access; update the Steam build to the latest version.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character to another area; reconnect to temporarily resolve it.
  • Characters may occasionally fall endlessly beneath the ground; Netmarble says a future update will address it.

Which issues were resolved?

The November 21 recommended update resolved the Act 1 Adventure Companion tutorial block, missing Ranking Boss challenge records, missing doors in the Cordin Reservoir Underground Waterway Administration Hall, and heroes intermittently missing from Team Formation. It also fixed certain Elite Boss effect displays in Cordin Underground Waterway and improved Armor menu sorting so equipped gear appears at the top. The November 25 data patch performed server stabilization work; fully close and reconnect to download it.

How should players troubleshoot safely?

Fully close and update the relevant platform before retrying. Reconnect only when the official notice names it as a temporary workaround. Do not delete saves, unlink accounts or install unofficial patches to work around a server-side issue. If the symptom differs from this list, record the platform, error text and time before contacting official support.
