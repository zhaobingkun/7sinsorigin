Swiftest Showdown Time Attack Event

November 4, 2026 at 07:00 UTC until the November 18 maintenance

Direct answer: Swiftest Showdown opens on November 4 at 07:00 UTC and runs until the November 18 maintenance. Its Time Attack Challenge content opens sequentially from Day 1 through Day 6.

What do I need to unlock it?

Complete the Main Quest “Open the Waterway”. Each day's challenge grants a Challenge Completion Reward, while the separate Time Attack missions award rewards for their own completion conditions.

What are the official maximum rewards?

  • Challenge Completion Rewards: Star Fragment ×1,800 and Gold ×4,800,000.
  • Time Attack mission rewards: Star Fragment ×600.
  • Total maximum listed across all challenges and missions: Star Fragment ×2,400.

The official notice says rewards cannot be claimed after the event ends. It does not publish a universal time ranking, character tier result or pull recommendation.

Official source: Swiftest Showdown - Time Attack Challenge!.
