Developer Notes #19: Discord AMA Season 3 Answers

Published: September 11, 2026 · Officially updated: September 11, 2026 11:00 UTC

Direct answer: Developer Notes #19 is an official response to Discord AMA Season 3 and a forward-looking plan for Version 2.1 and later updates. It explains why Labyrinth was removed from Version 2.0, confirms the shape of the first Poll Draw rerun, and previews new Raids, Knighthood content, World Level 5, seasonal events, and Daily Mission changes.

Why was Labyrinth removed?

Netmarble says Labyrinth had reached its final development stage, but a final review found that its weekly choice-based structure could become drawn out and monotonous. The team chose to improve it before release rather than ship content they believed could disappoint players. The notice apologizes for the late communication and does not give a return date.

What is planned for Version 2.1?

Version 2.1 is planned to add Vanya Village Ale Festival events and minigames, the Ferzen Mines Raid, and permanent Knighthood expansion. The Knighthood Headquarters will let members gather in a private area, customize designated spaces, craft decorations from donated materials, and potentially unlock functions such as daily dishes and Knighthood Boss Practice Mode. Story Replay and [Transcendence] difficulty for the Corrupted Ancient Dragon are also listed.

How will the Poll Draw rerun work?

All three heroes selected through the Poll Draw Survey are planned to be available at the same time during the Version 2.1 period. The existing Pick Up rules remain: an SSR within 80 draws, a 50% Pick Up chance, and the 120th Draw Bonus. For this rerun, players can choose one of the three as their Pick Up hero, change that choice during the draw, and select which hero to receive for the 120th bonus. The notice does not name the three heroes or give an exact date.

What future content is confirmed as planned?

The roadmap lists a new Underground Waterway Raid with water-level and diving mechanics, Timespace Junction Extra Sectors with hero-use limits, and a split between standard and ranking difficulties planned for October. World Level 5 is also being prepared with periodically berserk Field Bosses and occasional 15-player Field Events. Later seasonal plans include a new Knighthood Boss, a new 15-player Event Boss, and Halloween and winter-holiday events.

What changes are planned for Daily Missions?

A Daily Mission revamp is planned for October. Completing enough missions will grant Summon Scrolls that can eventually be exchanged for an SSR hero. Missions will span different regions, and region-specific Seal rewards and Seal Merchant inventories will be expanded. The announcement also says the team is preparing easier party-play balance and more ways to obtain Cube Keys.

What did the AMA say about hero reruns?

Regular hero reruns are planned after the Poll Draw rerun. They will use a separate schedule rather than simply following original release order, and the final format is still under discussion. When a hero returns, its Exclusive Skin Draw will return too; a skin already obtained will remain marked as completed.

What Raid and Timespace changes are being considered?

Netmarble plans to gradually increase weekly Mystic Keys, review lower-difficulty Raid rewards so they can use Cube Keys, and consider easing some same-Attribute Pledge requirements. The team is also reviewing party traversal tools, equipment filters, sorting, and clearer crafted-Engraved Equipment tracking. Timespace Junction Season 2 is intended to be easier than Season 1, while Extra Sectors and a separate ranking difficulty are planned for stronger competition.

What is the thank-you gift?

  • Star Fragment x3,000
  • Transcendence Refinement Stone x30
  • Gold x5,000,000
  • Cube Key x100
  • Claim from the in-game mailbox until October 7, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What is not confirmed?

This Developer Notes does not confirm a Version 2.1 release date, the names of the three Poll Draw heroes, a new banner rate or pity rule, or a date for Labyrinth. It is a roadmap and AMA response, not a new current-banner notice. Keep future content timing and banner decisions tied to later official notices.
