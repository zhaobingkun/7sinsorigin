Holy Knights and Thieves: The Great Chase Through a Lawless City

Published: September 3, 2026 · Updated: September 3, 2026 08:22 UTC

Direct answer: Holy Knights and Thieves is a Ravens minigame running after the September 3 maintenance until the September 16 maintenance in UTC. Players are randomly assigned as pursuers or thieves in a best-of-three match.

How do the Ravens roles work?

  • Enter through NPC Dormu from the event tab; you can enter solo or with a party of up to 5.
  • The match needs 10–15 players, with up to 4 Holy Knights and up to 11 Thieves.
  • Thieves collect and stash coins to raise the Crime Rate Gauge; Holy Knights lower it by defeating Thieves.
  • Round winners are decided by the Crime Rate when the time limit ends, and a round can draw.

Which missions and rewards are confirmed?

The mission track has Daily Missions plus role-specific Holy Knight and Thief Missions. Completing all Daily Missions rewards up to Unstolen Coin x780; the two role-specific tracks each reward up to Unstolen Coin x250. Daily Missions reset at 07:00 UTC.

What can Unstolen Coins buy?

The event shop includes Hellfire Keeper's Gauntlets, Brilliant Engraving Seal, Magic-infused Enhanced Metal, an Element Spell Bead Selection Chest, Platinum Ingots, Large Cube Key Bundles, Special Mastery EXP, enhancement and refinement stones, and Gold. Coins reset after the event ends, and leaving before the minigame ends does not count Win or Defeat objectives.

Official source: Holy Knights and Thieves.
