Hawk Pass Season V Rewards

Official notice published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:05 UTC

Direct answer: Hawk Pass Season V opens after the September 17 update. It has separate Free and Plus tracks with 50 levels each; existing Hawk Pass levels and mission progress reset when the new season starts.

What are the headline rewards?

  • Level 5 Plus: SSR Crafted Weapon Selection Chest and SSR Magic Crystal Shard x5.
  • Level 10 Plus: Life Crystal Full Set Chest and Tier Up Stone Selection Chest x3.
  • Level 15 Plus: Breath of Glory Weapon Selection Chest and Ingot Selection Chest x3.
  • Level 30 Plus: Hero Pick Up Draw Ticket x3 and Remnants of the Rift x5.

What do Plus and Premium add?

Hawk Pass Plus adds Star Fragment x1,200 and Loyalty x125. Premium adds the Plus rewards, an immediate 20-level increase up to level 50, Star Fragment x1,200, Regular Hero Draw Ticket x3, Small Cube Key Bundle x7, and Loyalty x250. Unclaimed rewards are sent to the mailbox at the end.

How do I level it?

Daily, weekly, and seasonal missions grant Hawk Pass XP. The Level Up button can also use 200 Star Fragments per level. Detailed purchase and reset timing is shown in-game.

Official source: Hawk Pass Season V Open.
