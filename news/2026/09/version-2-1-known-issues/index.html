Version 2.1 Known Issues Updated September 22

Published: September 17, 2026 · Updated: September 22, 2026 11:37 UTC

Direct answer: After the September 22 patch, the current Version 2.1 issue list keeps Khala’s Copycat passive, Hawk Pass XP, Hero Tag overlap, Skip input, controller navigation, Steam access, party movement, and falling-under-ground problems. The patch resolved the earlier Indura Monspeet, Vanya, Timespace, Hero Pick Up, Windflower, and three PS5 issues.

Current Version 2.1 issues

  • Khala’s Dual Swords passive “Copycat” can activate when tagging Khala with fewer than three Shaper effects.
  • Hawk Pass Mission rewards may apply the wrong amount of Hawk Pass XP.
  • Hero UI elements such as nicknames may intermittently overlap when a party member uses Hero Tag repeatedly.
  • The Skip the Main Quest key input may not work in some PC or gamepad situations.
  • The controller cursor may disappear after viewing a material Set Effect on the Engraved Equipment crafting screen.
  • Steam error 30021225-0 may block access; updating the Steam build to the latest version is the official workaround.
  • Leaving a party at a specific location may move the character elsewhere; reconnecting can temporarily resolve it, and characters may occasionally fall beneath the ground.

Resolved on September 22

The official notice marks the Indura Monspeet Hell Transcendence Essence drop, Vanya Village Day 4 progression, Timespace Season 2 labels and difficulty colors, Hero Pick Up Draw button, Windflower updraft crash, and three PlayStation 5 freeze or interaction issues as resolved. The September 17 PS5 120th Draw Bonus and September 21 Vanya Day 5 hint fixes remain recorded as resolved.

What should you do?

Fully exit, install the latest platform update, reconnect, and retry. Do not treat a remaining issue as resolved unless Netmarble marks it resolved in a later notice.

Official source: Version 2.1 Known Issues.
