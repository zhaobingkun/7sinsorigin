Version 2.1 Known Issues Updated September 21

Published: September 17, 2026 · Updated: September 21, 2026 09:19 UTC

Direct answer: The current Version 2.1 issue list includes Khala’s Copycat passive triggering incorrectly, missing Transcendence Essence in the Indura Monspeet Hell challenge, several PS5 freezes or interaction problems, Vanya Village Day 4 progression, Hawk Pass XP, Timespace labels, controller navigation, Steam access, party movement, and falling beneath the ground.

New or current Version 2.1 issues

  • Khala’s Dual Swords passive “Copycat” can activate when tagging Khala with fewer than three Shaper effects.
  • Transcendence Essence may not drop in the Hell difficulty of the Indura Monspeet Boss Challenge.
  • On PlayStation 5, crafting material selection, Field Boss Equipment Rewards set-effect checks, and some quest mechanics or objects may make the game unresponsive.
  • Vanya Village Ale Festival Day 4 may intermittently fail to progress.
  • Hawk Pass Mission rewards may apply the wrong amount of Hawk Pass XP.
  • Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 3 and 4 leaderboard labels, and the difficulty-color UI for Sectors 5 and 6, may display incorrectly.

Other tracked issues

The notice also keeps the controller cursor, Steam error 30021225-0, party movement, falling-under-ground, repeated Hero Tag overlap, Hero Pick Up Draw button, and Windflower updraft crash issues on the list. The Steam workaround is to update to the latest Steam build; reconnecting may temporarily fix the party-location issue.

Resolved items

The PS5 120th Draw Bonus issue was resolved on September 17, and the Vanya Village Day 5 hint was corrected on September 21.

What should you do?

Fully exit, install the latest platform update, reconnect, and retry. Do not treat an issue as resolved unless Netmarble marks it resolved in a later notice.

Official source: Version 2.1 Known Issues.
