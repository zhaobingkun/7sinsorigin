Raid Missions: Conqueror's Path

Official event notice published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:05 UTC

Direct answer: Conqueror's Path opens after the September 17 maintenance and runs until the October 8 maintenance. It combines challenge missions for Version 2.1 content with Raid Missions for Ferzen Mines, the new Raid.

What rewards are confirmed?

  • Challenge missions: Star Fragment x600, Transcendence Refinement Stone x35, Transcendence Engraving Stone x20, Special Refinement Stone x50, Special Enhancement Stone x20, Token Selection Chest x2, Special Mastery EXP x20, Gold x500,000, and crafting materials.
  • All Raid Missions: Mystic Key x5.
  • Final completion: Hero Pick Up Draw Ticket x5.

What should I clear first?

Complete the Main Quest “HP Recovery!” to enter the event, then clear the listed challenge and Raid missions before the event ends. Rewards must be claimed during the event window; the final ticket is tied to full Raid Mission progress.

How does it connect to Version 2.1?

The event supports the new Ferzen Mines Raid and the Transcendence-difficulty Corrupted Ancient Dragon Boss Challenge. Use the separate Raid Guide for general raid planning, while this page records the time-limited mission rewards.
