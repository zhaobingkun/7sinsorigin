September 10 Server Patch: Transcendence Leaderboard Closure

Published: September 10, 2026 · Patch completed: September 10, 2026 02:31 UTC · Updated: September 10, 2026 02:35 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble completed the September 10 server patch at 02:31 UTC. It fixed several Boss Challenge and Field Boss issues, but it also closed the [Transcendence] leaderboards for Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet and Event Boss Challenge, Her Return because fair ranking could no longer be ensured.

Why were the Transcendence leaderboards closed?

The official notice identified an unintended problem with the Boss Challenge Restart feature. In party play, tagging a hero while waiting to retry could make the next attempt begin from the tagged location. Joining an ongoing Boss Challenge through a party invitation could also register a clear record for every party member. Netmarble closed the two affected leaderboards while fixing those issues.

Will ranking rewards be distributed?

No. Ranking-placement rewards for the two closed leaderboards will not be distributed. Instead, eligible players can receive apology compensation by clearing either affected Boss Challenge on [Transcendence] difficulty in solo play before the September 16 maintenance.

What is the apology compensation?

  • Clear Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet on Transcendence: Laurel of Glory x20.
  • Clear Her Return on Transcendence: Transcendence Engraving Stone x10 and Transcendence Refinement Stone x25.
  • Compensation is delivered through the in-game mailbox during the September 16 maintenance and can be claimed until October 8, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What other bugs did the patch fix?

The patch fixed cases where a Field Boss Reward Cube animation played without granting rewards and where selecting World Level 4 could still grant World Level 1 rewards. Temporary disconnections may occur during sequential patch application, so reconnect before retrying affected content.

What happens to future Transcendence time attacks?

Transcendence difficulty Time Attacks remain available. Until the system improvements are complete, however, there will be no Titles or reward differences based on ranking placement; all participants will receive the same rewards. This notice does not announce a new banner or change Ban's current pickup.
