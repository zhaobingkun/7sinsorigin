Version 2.1 Maintenance Schedule

Official schedule published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:04 UTC

Direct answer: Version 2.1 maintenance is scheduled for September 17, 2026 from 02:00 to 08:00 UTC. The game will be unavailable during the six-hour window, which was moved from September 16 for stability.

What compensation is confirmed?

  • Maintenance compensation: Star Fragment x300.
  • Players who clear Indura Monspeet or Her Return on Transcendence in solo play before maintenance receive the listed apology rewards through the in-game mailbox.
  • Indura Monspeet clear: Laurel of Glory x20.
  • Her Return clear: Transcendence Engraving Stone x10 and Transcendence Refinement Stone x25.

What arrives with Version 2.1?

The notice lists Khala, her exclusive skin, the Selective Pick Up, new Ferzen Mines Raid, Corrupted Ancient Dragon Transcendence difficulty, Knighthood Headquarters and decorating, Ranking Season 5, Hawk Pass Season V, and four new event tracks.

What ends or changes?

Ban Pick Up, Ban's trial and skin, Holy Knights and Thieves, Indura Monspeet Transcendence, Lucky Snatch, and Ranking Season 4 end around the maintenance. Her Return is extended to September 23 at 06:59:59 UTC. Ban is therefore current until this maintenance; Khala is not current before it completes.

Official source: Version 2.1 Maintenance Schedule.
