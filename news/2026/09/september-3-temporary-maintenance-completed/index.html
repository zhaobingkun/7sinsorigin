September 3 Temporary Maintenance Completed

Published: September 1, 2026 · Completed and updated: September 3, 2026 09:20 UTC

Direct answer: The official September 3 temporary maintenance is completed, and the game is available. The final maintenance window was 03:00–09:20 UTC.

What opened after maintenance?

  • Event Boss - Her Return: fight the returned Liones Vice Grandmaster Guila and exchange Mutated Magic Bead Tokens for rewards.
  • Holy Knights and Thieves: play in Ravens as one of 4 Holy Knights or 11 Thieves and exchange Unstolen Coins.
  • Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 1: the new season is available after this maintenance.

What changed from the Version 2.0 plan?

The Labyrinth content announced for Version 2.0 was excluded from this maintenance while it receives further review. Netmarble says its release schedule will be announced later, so this page does not treat Labyrinth as live.

What did the maintenance fix?

The notice covers Act 12 and Act 16 quest fixes, Deep Spider Nest boss positioning, controller chat, Engraved Equipment refinement warnings and restoration, Codex source information, pet and puzzle issues, Oasis Village minimap icons, mobile Lucky Snatch animation, and other UI or skill fixes. Elaine's Grimoire Tier 10 description was also corrected without changing the actual skill effect.

Compensation and next steps

Claim Star Fragment x300 from the in-game mailbox after maintenance and before September 17, 2026 at 14:59 UTC. Reconnect and update before retrying content, then review the latest Version 2.0 Known Issues page if a problem remains.

Official source: September 3 Temporary Maintenance.
