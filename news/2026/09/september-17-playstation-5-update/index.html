September 17 PS5 Update Fixes the 120th Draw Bonus

Published: September 17, 2026 · Update applied: September 17, 2026 14:00 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble applied a no-maintenance PlayStation 5 update at 14:00 UTC on September 17 to fix the issue where the 120th Draw Bonus could not be selected in Selective Pick Up – Stargazer Invitation.

What did the update fix?

The update addresses the PS5-specific Selective Pick Up 120th Draw Bonus selection problem. It does not announce a new banner rule or change the bonus itself.

What should PS5 players do?

Fully exit the game and install the latest platform update. If the update does not appear, refresh the platform and try again. Players may be temporarily disconnected during the rollout; reconnect afterward.

What remains separate?

Other PS5 freezes and interaction problems remain listed in the current Version 2.1 Known Issues notice.

Official source: September 17 PlayStation 5 Update.
