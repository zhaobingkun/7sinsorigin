Vanya Village Ale Festival Guide

Official event notice published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:05 UTC

Direct answer: Vanya Village Ale Festival opens after the September 17 maintenance and runs until the October 8 maintenance in UTC. Complete quests, missions, and the ale minigame to earn Empty Mugs for the event shop.

How do I unlock it?

Complete the Main Quest “Enter: Meliodas...?” to access the event. The five event quests unlock over five days. Clearing “Tavern, First Day” unlocks the minigame, which can be played up to three times daily; attempts reset at 07:00 UTC.

How does the ale minigame work?

Three of eight ale types are selected at random. Match the order, then use Pour Ale and Pour Foam to fill the mug close to its guide lines. Each button can be used once per mug. Better accuracy produces a higher score and more Empty Mugs.

What can Empty Mugs buy?

The exchange shop includes The Princess's Resolve outfit for SSR Elizabeth, a Brilliant Engraving Seal, Transcendence Engraving Stones, Transcendence Refinement Stones, SSR Magic Crystal Shards, cube keys, mastery EXP, and Gold. Empty Mugs reset when the event ends, and rewards cannot be claimed afterward.
