Developer Notes #20: Version 2.2 and Future Plans

Published: September 23, 2026 · Officially checked: September 23, 2026 11:30 UTC

Direct answer: Developer Notes #20 is an official feedback follow-up, not a final release schedule. It confirms ongoing PS5 and Ferzen Mines stability work, planned Version 2.2 improvements such as Bag expansion, hero reruns, Engraved Equipment salvage rewards, and a new support-focused weapon set, plus longer-term combat and open-world plans.

What is being investigated now?

Netmarble says it is investigating Version 2.1 PlayStation 5 control issues and crashes, especially stuttering and crashes in Ferzen Mines. The team is also reviewing mobile and platform-specific controls, Dishes usability, gameplay stability, and monster-stat visibility.

What is planned for Version 2.2?

  • Bag expansion and additional salvage rewards for Engraved Equipment are planned.
  • SSR Engraved Equipment salvage is planned to grant materials such as Transcendence Refinement Stones; SR salvage is planned to grant fragments for Brilliant Engraving Seals.
  • Pick Up reruns are planned to begin, with one older SSR hero featured during each version period.
  • More opportunities to exchange for previously released SSR Special Weapon Crafting Blueprints are planned.
  • A new support-focused weapon set is planned for Version 2.2, targeted for the second half of 2026.

What future combat and world plans were confirmed?

The next hero is planned for Version 2.2 with dodge and Perfect Charge-related combat elements. Netmarble also targets Timespace Junction boss-spawn adjustments for Version 2.3, equipment filter improvements for Version 2.4, a fishing tournament for Version 2.4, and seafaring content sometime next year. These are plans, not guaranteed dates.

What is the AMA thank-you gift?

The gift contains Transcendence Refinement Stone x30, Mystic Key x1, Special HP Recovery Potion x30, and Platinum Ingot x30. It can be claimed from the in-game mailbox until October 27, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

What is not confirmed?

The notice does not provide a final Version 2.2 release date, exact rerun hero, final weapon stats, or a complete implementation schedule. Do not treat the roadmap as a current banner announcement.
