Khala Pick Up and Exclusive Skin

Official notice published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:07 UTC

Direct answer: Khala Pick Up – Ravens's Thief of Honor runs after the September 17 maintenance until the October 8 maintenance in UTC. The notice confirms an increased SSR Khala rate and Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

What are the Khala banner rules?

  • Complete the Main Quest “HP Recovery!” to enter.
  • An SSR is guaranteed within 80 draws, with a 50% chance for the Pick Up hero.
  • If the Pick Up hero is missed, the next SSR is guaranteed to be Khala.
  • Hero Pick Up draw count and guaranteed Pick Up status carry to another Hero Pick Up.
  • The 120th Draw Bonus guarantees Khala once per banner and does not carry over.

What does the Khala trial give?

The fully upgraded trial lets players test Khala and awards Star Fragment x300, Supreme Mastery EXP x30, Unnamed Knight's Token x4, Bigmoss Young Flower x2, Vivid Wild Token x2, Rough Giant Bird Feather x2, and Mermaid's Tear x2.

What is the exclusive skin?

A Belief Called Honor can award Khala's outfit skin A Grand Thief's Dignity and the Dual Swords weapon skin Python Fangs. The first draw is free, rewards do not duplicate, and the full reward pool is guaranteed within 10 draws. Skins change appearance only and do not change stats or skills.

Official source: Khala Pick Up & Exclusive Skin.
