Selective Pick Up: Stargazer Invitation

Official notice published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:07 UTC

Direct answer: Stargazer Invitation opens after the September 17 maintenance and runs until the October 8 maintenance. You select Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy as the Pick Up hero; the other two do not appear in that draw.

How does selection work?

  • Complete Main Quest “HP Recovery!”.
  • Set or change your Pick Up choice at any time; the new choice applies from the next draw.
  • Your accumulated Hero Pick Up count and 120th Draw Bonus gauge stay intact when changing.
  • The normal 80-draw SSR guarantee and 50% selected-hero chance remain.
  • At 120 draws, choose any one of the three heroes as the one-time bonus; this bonus is valid only for this event.

What other skin draws return?

Merlin's Sacred Treasure: Morning Star Aldan, Elizabeth's Resonance of a Noble Heart, and Daisy's Fresh Scent of Autumn also return. Each starts with a free draw, has no duplicate rewards, and guarantees its full reward pool within 10 draws. Previous progress carries over for the returning Merlin, Elizabeth, and Daisy skin draws.

Official source: Selective Pick Up - Stargazer Invitation.
