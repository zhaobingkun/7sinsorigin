Update Eve Check-In Event Guide

Official event notice published September 23, 2026 · updated September 23, 2026 07:02 UTC

Direct answer: Update Eve is a 14-day check-in event from September 23 at 07:00 UTC until the October 8 maintenance. Complete the Main Quest HP Recovery!, then log in once per day; the reward track resets at 07:00 UTC.

What are the 14 rewards?

  • Day 1: Special Mastery EXP x30
  • Day 2: Hero Pick Up Draw Ticket x3
  • Day 3: Ingot Selection Chest x5
  • Day 4: Gold x500,000
  • Day 5: Regular Hero Draw Ticket x5
  • Day 6: Large Cube Key Bundle x3
  • Day 7: Brilliant Engraving Seal x1
  • Day 8: Radiant Potion of Charm x3
  • Day 9: Large Cube Key Bundle x3
  • Day 10: Hero Pick Up Draw Ticket x2
  • Day 11: Ingot Selection Chest x5
  • Day 12: Large Cube Key Bundle x3
  • Day 13: Gold x1,000,000
  • Day 14: Brilliant Engraving Seal x1

Can I claim rewards after the event?

No. The official notice says rewards cannot be claimed after the event ends, so collect each unlocked day during the event period.
