Developer Notes #19.5: Daisy and Version 2.2 Plans

Published: September 16, 2026 · Officially checked: September 16, 2026 11:03 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble plans to make Daisy obtainable through the revamped Daily Mission system targeted for Version 2.2. Developer Notes #19.5 also targets weapon Enchantment and Engraving Bonus reroll improvements for Version 2.3, Bag expansion, more Seal Merchant weapon access, and 40 additional Cube Keys per day through a giveaway until Version 2.2.

What is planned for Daisy?

Daisy is the first hero Netmarble plans to make available through the revamped Daily Mission system. The notice describes a guaranteed path earned by completing Daily Missions consistently. It does not give the final launch date, exact mission rewards, or the full future hero list.

What changes are planned for Version 2.2?

The Daily Mission revamp is targeted for Version 2.2. Netmarble also plans to expand Seal rewards across more villages and update Seal Merchant inventories so General-grade weapons can be obtained through additional exchanges. More Cube Keys are also planned through Daily Missions.

What changes are targeted for Version 2.3?

The team is currently targeting Version 2.3 for improvements to rerolling weapon Enchantments and Engraving Bonuses on Engraved Equipment. Implementation details are still under discussion and are not confirmed as final.

What else is changing?

  • Additional Bag capacity is planned at no cost, with further expansion options through products such as subscriptions and Battle Passes.
  • Selective Pick Up heroes retain their original Star Trace Crystalloid exchange status.
  • Version 2.1 eased some Raid Pledge conditions and added weekly Transcendence stone exchanges to the Raid Shop.
  • A giveaway plans to provide 40 additional Cube Keys each day from after the September 17 update until Version 2.2.

What is the thank-you gift?

Players can claim Star Fragment x3,000 from the in-game mailbox until October 7, 2026 at 14:59 UTC, as a thank-you for Origin being selected as one of the 2026 Best Games of the Year by KOCCA.

What is not confirmed?

This is a development plan, not a final patch schedule. Exact Version 2.2 or 2.3 dates, final rewards, and implementation details remain unconfirmed until a later official notice.
