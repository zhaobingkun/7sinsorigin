September 22 Server Patch Fixes Vanya, Indura, and PS5 Issues

Published and completed: September 22, 2026 · Patch window: 11:00–11:35 UTC

Direct answer: Netmarble completed the September 22 server patch and update at 11:35 UTC without maintenance. Players should fully exit the game, install the platform update, and reconnect.

What did the patch fix?

  • Fixed certain crash issues and the intermittent Vanya Village Ale Festival Day 4 story-mission progression issue.
  • Restored Transcendence Essence drops in the Hell difficulty of the Beast of Destruction, Indura Monspeet Boss Challenge.
  • Corrected Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 3 and 4 leaderboard names and the difficulty-color UI for Sectors 5 and 6.
  • Fixed the Hero Pick Up Draw button becoming unavailable after viewing Hero Info and a Skill popup.
  • Fixed crashes when using a Windflower updraft while riding a pet.
  • Improved mobile Powder Keg visibility in the Ferzen Mines Raid and reduced Powder Kegs during certain mechanics.
  • Fixed mobile hero-icon positioning, certain hero graphics with a Gauntlets Weapon Skin, and PlayStation 5 crafting, Field Boss reward, and quest-interaction freezes.

What should players do?

This was a no-maintenance, sequential platform update. Completely exit the game, install the latest update, and reconnect. Availability can vary by platform and connection; refresh and retry if the update is not shown.

How does this affect known issues?

The official Version 2.1 Known Issues notice marks the Vanya Day 4, Indura Monspeet, Timespace, Hero Pick Up, Windflower, and three PS5 issues as resolved on September 22. Khala’s Copycat passive, Hawk Pass XP, Hero Tag overlap, Skip input, controller cursor, Steam access, party movement, and falling-under-ground issues remain listed.

Official source: September 22 Server Patch & Update.
