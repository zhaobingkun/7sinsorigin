Timespace Junction - Season 2

Published: September 3, 2026 · Updated: September 3, 2026 08:22 UTC

Direct answer: Timespace Junction Season 2 is a solo seasonal mode available after the September 3 maintenance. Season 2 runs until the January 6, 2027 maintenance in UTC, with Sector 1 open first and Sector 2 scheduled for September 9 at 07:00 UTC.

How do you unlock Season 2?

Complete Main Quest Act 13 - Episode 25, “Reunion”. Enter from Timespace Junction in the Menu or through the portal near its World Map location.

What are the rules?

  • The mode does not support party play, entry tickets, or entry limits.
  • Clear Sectors in order; the previous Sector must be cleared before the next one can be challenged.
  • Each Sector has Recommended and Disadvantaged Elements, and Recommended Heroes provide seasonal effects.
  • Your best clear time updates when you beat your previous record.

How do rewards work?

Each Sector offers a First Clear Reward and three tiers of Mission Rewards based on clear time. Rewards can be claimed once per season, and claim records reset when a new season begins. Check the Sector information panel and reward icon in-game for the exact current thresholds.
