Version 2.1 Update Details: Khala and Ferzen Mines

Official update details published September 15, 2026 · updated September 15, 2026 10:08 UTC

Direct answer: Version 2.1 adds new SSR hero Khala, her Ravens pickup and skin, the selective Stargazer Invitation rerun, Ferzen Mines, Knighthood Headquarters, new events, and Hawk Pass Season V. The associated maintenance is scheduled for September 17, 02:00–08:00 UTC.

What is new?

  • Khala uses Dual Swords, Nunchaku, and Gauntlets; her official hero page lists an Adventure Skill that can obtain Reward Cubes three times per day when she is on the team.
  • Ferzen Mines opens with Nightmare, Hell, and Abyss difficulties, with new support-oriented equipment and first-clear rewards.
  • Knighthood Headquarters adds a shared member space, decorating, donations, Knighthood Coins, and planned utility functions such as daily dishes or Boss Practice Mode tied to some decorations.
  • Corrupted Ancient Dragon gains Transcendence difficulty, and Story Replay is added.

Which limited events begin after maintenance?

Khala's trial, Vanya Village Ale Festival, Conqueror's Path, Lucky Snatch II, the Stargazer Invitation, and returning Merlin, Elizabeth, and Daisy skin draws are listed. Additional check-in, time-attack, and Koo story content begins September 23.

What is confirmed about the rerun?

Stargazer Invitation lets players choose Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy, change the choice during the event, and select any of the three for the 120th Draw Bonus. The official notice confirms the event format and names; it does not turn this into a universal meta ranking.

What should players do before September 17?

Ban Pick Up, Ban's trial and skin, Holy Knights and Thieves, Lucky Snatch, and Indura Monspeet Transcendence end around the maintenance. Check the maintenance schedule and claim eligible rewards before the changeover.
