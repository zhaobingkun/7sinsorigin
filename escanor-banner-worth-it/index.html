Is Escanor Worth Pulling in Seven Deadly Sins Origin?

Escanor is worth pulling when your account can actually support a heavier carry route. If the roster is still unstable, the smarter move is often protecting diamonds and team flexibility until the rest of the account catches up.

Version 1.6 Buff Confirmed

Developer Notes #14 confirms that Escanor's Axe and Greatsword activation conditions will be eased, their utility will improve, and his maximum damage potential will rise. Exact values are not published yet, so treat this as a stronger future signal rather than a finished tier-list verdict.

Read Developer Notes #14

Pull If

  • Your account already has a stable shell and can afford a higher-cost carry route
  • Escanor gives immediate value in your current June roster plan instead of forcing a full rebuild
  • You can still keep pity and diamond recovery under control after one live pull cycle

Save If

Save if your roster is still missing core stability, if your diamonds are thin, or if pulling Escanor now would force the account into an expensive route it cannot actually finish.
