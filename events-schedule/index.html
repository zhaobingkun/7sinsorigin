Seven Deadly Sins Origin Events Schedule

Event-schedule pages matter because players do not only need news headlines. They need a stable place to check which campaigns, login windows, and limited reward tracks are worth caring about right now.

Upcoming: Version 1.8 Support Rewards

Developer Notes #17 says Version 1.8 will add a limited-time 15-player Hunting Event, Memory of Dimensions, renewed growth missions, and QoL changes. Regular Hero Draw Ticket x10 will be sent every Monday at 09:00 UTC for five weeks, and each delivery expires after seven days.

Developer Notes #17 Version 1.8 Preview

Completed: July 28 Server Patch

The July 28 server patch completed at 05:32 UTC without maintenance and fixed intermittent Confirmed! Twigo daily-reset failures. Affected players received 10 event tickets per impacted day on July 29 at 09:00 UTC.

Twigo Reset Fix and Ticket Compensation

Completed: July 22 Temporary Maintenance

The July 22 temporary maintenance completed at 08:00 UTC. It opened Knighthood Boss Battle, Knighthood rankings, Knighthood Shop, Timespace Junction Sector 9, and the Confirmed! Twigo event. Compensation is Star Fragment x300, claimable until August 5 at 14:59 UTC.

July 22 Temporary Maintenance Completed

Completed: Confirmed! Twigo

Confirmed! Twigo ran after the July 22 maintenance until the August 5 maintenance. The event unlocked after Main Quest HP Recovery!, used tickets from seven mission phases, and gave Fever Star Fragment x400 rewards up to four times. Its daily reset issue was fixed, with ticket compensation sent on July 29.

Confirmed! Twigo Event Details

Completed: July 16 Data Patch

The July 16 data patch completed at 16:18 UTC. It adjusts Catapult behavior, removes Gold from the new Elite Monster Werewolf Chieftain reward list, and decreases normal enemy HP in Deep Spider Nest. Fully close and reconnect to apply the new data.
