Bingo: inspect missing numbers before selecting an exchange

Official limits: up to 7 Bingo Tickets per day from Daily Missions, up to 15 Chance Coins from all Chance Missions, and up to 36 Bingo Tickets from all Call Missions. Duplicate numbers return one Chance Coin. Three Chance Coins buy one selected number.

Editorial procedure: check the numbers missing from your current group and whether existing coins can fill them directly. Random draws are not a guaranteed completion plan. Group 3 completion includes Star Fragment ×1,000; this is not a fixed reward for each bingo line.

Check-In: today's choice changes timing, not the final full-track total

Choose one of two rewards each day. Claiming all 14 days also grants the rewards not selected earlier. If you finish the whole track, today's choice determines what you receive sooner; it does not permanently forfeit the other reward. Day 2 includes Hero Pick Up Draw Ticket ×3 and Day 10 includes ×2. Do not count a selected reward again as an unchosen completion reward.

Check-In and Bingo Daily Missions explicitly reset at 07:00 UTC. That does not establish a universal reset time for every system. Their entry requirement is Main Quest HP Recovery!.

Check-In coverage · Official rules (329)

Cube: maximum mission rewards are not per-box income

The notice lists maximum mission totals of Cube Key ×285 and Regular Hero Draw Ticket ×10. Final completion rewards are Transcendence Engraving Stone ×15 and Transcendence Refinement Stone ×75. These are not rewards from every opened cube, and regular draw tickets are not Pick Up tickets.

Editorial procedure: inspect your remaining mission thresholds and claim buttons before committing extra keys. The notice does not reveal every account's remaining cost; a large total reward is not proof that further spending is worthwhile.
