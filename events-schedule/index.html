What changed from the earlier schedule?

Netmarble moved the temporary maintenance from November 4 to November 5. The events and Timespace Junction Season 2 Sector 7 opened on November 4 at 07:00 UTC instead, while World Level 5, Star Map and the Demonic Beast Akumu revamp arrived with the completed maintenance. The follow-up notice confirms Gray Demon and Red Demon are included in Massive Corruption Frenzy.

Maintenance compensation is Star Fragment ×300, and the World Level 5 celebration adds 3 Corruption Frenzy Activation Tickets; both are claimable from the mailbox until November 19 at 14:59 UTC. Chaos That Devours the Desert ended before the maintenance on November 4 at 06:59:59 UTC.

What should banner players check?

Estia and Clotho use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets and are announced through the November 18 maintenance. The official notices confirm the window and increased featured-hero rates; use the live Rate Info panel for exact rules, then record your ticket budget and separate one-time 120th Draw Bonus progress.
