What should players claim first?

Log in daily for the 14-day check-in. Each day lets you choose one reward, and completing all 14 days grants the unchosen rewards as an additional bonus. Complete Conqueror’s Path before the December 9 maintenance if you want its final five Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets.

For the Hawk mini-game, daily missions reset at 07:00 UTC. Fishing missions and the Event Boss also use daily limits, so do not leave event currency or mailbox rewards until the last minute.

What changed for Raid planning?

Cordin Underground Waterway opens with Hell, Abyss and Transcendence after Act 17 Episode 75. Version 2.4 resets Mystic Keys, Chaos Keys and weekly reward claim counts according to the official notice; exchange-shop purchase limits do not reset. The standard Mystic Key replenishment limit is now 3.

How should you handle later deadlines?

Maintenance timing can move. Recheck the in-game event panel and the latest official notice before spending tickets, keys or event currency. Older Lancelot, Elaine, Estia and Clotho pages remain dated archive records.
