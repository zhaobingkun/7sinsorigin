What should Version 2.3 players check?

Raid keys and weekly reward claims reset during the October 28 maintenance, with post-update replenishment based on the amount used before the update. The October 30 data patch fixed the Mighty Freaky Circus maze-exit entrance issue; fully close and reconnect if the patch has not applied.

The same patch improved targeting detection for chess pieces in Taranis's Sanctuary and significantly reduced their HP. Read the current Version 2.3 issue and patch coverage before reinstalling or changing account settings.

What should banner players check?

Estia and Clotho use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets and are announced through the November 18 maintenance. The official notices confirm the window and increased featured-hero rates; use the live Rate Info panel for the exact rules, then record your ticket budget and separate one-time 120th Draw Bonus progress.
