What should Hunting Event players check?

Complete Act 12 - Episode 15 “Corrupted” Ancient Dragon, progress through the five sequential event quests, then clear Beings of Deep Darkness to open the Kalbash Hunt. The Abyss dungeon supports 10–15 players, and up to two Desert Keeper’s Keys reset daily at 07:00 UTC.

What should Knighthood participants check?

Articles 340 and 341 remove affected Boss Battle records, postpone ranking rewards, and give the same ranking rewards to all participants when distributed. The follow-up also resolves the failed attempt and individual-score reset. Individual cumulative-score rewards are recalculated after the stated resets, with up to 15 Knighthood Coins total under the notice’s conditions.
