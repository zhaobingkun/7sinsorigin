Seven Deadly Sins Origin Twitch Drops: Version 1.7 Rewards

The current Twitch Drops campaign starts after the July 15, 2026 update maintenance and ends on August 9, 2026 at 14:59 UTC.

Direct Answer

Link your Origin game account with Twitch, watch Drops-enabled streams in the The Seven Deadly Sins: Origin category, claim the Drops in Twitch, then claim them on the event page for in-game mailbox delivery.

Read the official event summary

Watch-Time Rewards

  • 15 minutes: Supreme Mastery EXP x10
  • 30 minutes: Special Refinement Stone x10
  • 1 hour: Special Tier Up Stone x10
  • 1 hour 30 minutes: Cube Key Bundle x3
  • 2 hours: Regular Hero Draw Ticket x3

Claim Notes

  • Rewards go to the first server character accessed after linking.
  • Linked accounts cannot be changed after the first reward claim for the campaign.
  • Twitch Drops Inventory rewards expire if unclaimed for 10 days.
  • Event-page mailbox rewards are stored for 7 days.
