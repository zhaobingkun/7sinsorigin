7DS Origin Currency Budget: Tickets and Reward Limits

Separate the ticket types before adding up a pull budget. Unusable tickets and rewards that arrive too late cannot close a current-banner shortfall.

Editorial review: October 11, 2026. Official facts and our analysis are identified separately.

What can you actually count toward a Pick Up?

Use the ticket name printed on the item, not the word “diamonds” used in older guide URLs. The Lancelot notice specifies Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets. Regular Hero Draw Tickets, Bingo Tickets, Cube Keys and skin tickets are separate resources. This guide does not assume a Star Fragment-to-ticket exchange price; check the live shop before converting a balance.

A budget example without an invented conversion rate

Editorial example: the current banner shows 80/120 on its unclaimed bonus gauge, leaving 40 draws. Your wallet has 10 Hero Pick Up tickets and you can still claim the Check-In’s 3+2 ticket selections before the banner closes. That is at most 15 identified tickets, leaving a 25-ticket shortfall. Twenty Regular Hero Draw Tickets do not close it. Star Fragments only reduce the shortfall after you verify an actual exchange price and that you intend to make that exchange.

This is a resource ledger, not a promise of future income. If a reward has already been claimed, is inaccessible, or arrives after the banner ends, remove it from the estimate. For the separate SSR counter and guaranteed-Pick-Up state, use the worked pull-budget guide.

Use two columns: available now and conditional

  1. Record the exact item name and wallet quantity.
  2. Put unclaimed rewards in a separate column with unlock condition, quantity and expiry.
  3. Count only rewards you can obtain before the spending deadline; do not count mail until you can claim it.
  4. Subtract purchases you already decided to make elsewhere.
  5. Recheck the actual banner counters before spending. Stop at your planned goal; a nearly full bonus gauge is not a reason to buy more than you intended.

There is no measured “fastest farming route” on this site. A repeatable route would need a recorded version, access requirement, reward and time per run before we could compare efficiency honestly.
