Both notices give a window from after October 8 maintenance until October 28 maintenance. This is an announcement directory for the current update, not an exhaustive playable roster or a power ranking.

What a skill notice can establish

An official skill description can establish names, conditions and the described effects. It cannot by itself establish equal-investment damage rankings, clear times or the cheapest team. Lancelot's trial uses a fully upgraded hero with appearance skins; that is not a direct measurement of a new account's investment experience.

When reading a kit, record the trigger, duration, weapon and partner requirements separately. Check whether your own account can satisfy them. A maximum effect with unmet conditions is not an everyday performance estimate.

Separate account fit from acquisition budget

First identify the failure or goal you need to address. Then check the ticket budget needed to obtain the hero. Availability is not proof of value, and character preference is not a substitute for a spending plan. Use Investment priorities and pull-budget examples.

Older profiles with unverified role inferences and stale current-banner advice have been withdrawn. A republished build guide needs attributable kit evidence or a recorded version, loadout and test result. Story biography is not evidence of combat strength.
