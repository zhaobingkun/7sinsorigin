How 7sinsorigin.com Produces Its Content

Last reviewed: September 26, 2026 · Maintained by the 7sinsorigin.com Editorial Desk

This is an independent fan site, not an official Netmarble publication. Our job is to turn official announcements and practical player questions into readable, clearly sourced guides without pretending that rumors or roadmap ideas are confirmed facts.

What We Publish

Our main coverage is organized around decisions a player can actually make: understanding a patch, choosing a current banner, planning an account, solving a technical problem, or learning a system. A page should answer one clear question and provide a next step. We do not create pages only to target a keyword variation, repeat a short summary, or fill a directory.

Source Hierarchy

For current facts we start with Netmarble’s official game pages, notices, update details, and official social or video channels. We record the article date, update time, event window, platform, and reward conditions when the source confirms them. Search snippets, community posts, leaks, and inferred banner plans are not treated as confirmation.

Original Analysis

Official notices are the evidence, not the finished article. Our useful contribution is the explanation around them: who a change affects, what a player should do next, what remains uncertain, how a banner decision fits an account goal, and which related guide resolves the next problem. When a page cannot add that context, we keep it as an archive or leave it out of search.

How Updates Work

Live-service information changes. Current hubs and decision pages are refreshed when an official notice changes the answer. Older news pages keep their original patch or event context so the archive remains honest. A visible published or reviewed date is used where it helps readers understand the freshness of the advice.

Translations and Automation

English pages are the primary editorial record and Chinese pages are maintained under /zh/. Translations are edited for meaning and readability rather than copied word-for-word. We use structured templates and automation for navigation, metadata, and consistency checks, but factual selection, uncertainty labels, page pruning, and final wording require editorial review.

Corrections and Independence

We correct factual, source, translation, accessibility, and copyright problems when they are reported or discovered. 7sinsorigin.com is unofficial and independent; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netmarble or the owners of The Seven Deadly Sins. Advertising does not buy rankings, recommendations, or favorable coverage. Send a page URL and supporting detail through our Contact page.
