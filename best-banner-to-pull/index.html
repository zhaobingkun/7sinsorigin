Best Banner to Pull On

Ban is current until the September 17 maintenance. Khala and Selective Pick Up are confirmed after that maintenance, so the best choice depends on your account and timing.

Current Short Answer

Ban is the current choice only for an immediate account need. If you can wait, compare the confirmed Khala and Selective Pick Up plans.

Choose Ban If

You need Ban's role before September 17, have tested the trial, and accept the 80-draw SSR guarantee, 50% Pick Up chance, and carry-over rules.

Save for Khala If

You want the confirmed Khala Pick Up window after the September 17 maintenance through the October 8 maintenance. Read the official Khala details before assuming a universal meta ranking.

Consider Selective Pick Up If

You prefer to choose Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy after maintenance. The other two are excluded from the draw, and the selection can be changed before the next draw.
