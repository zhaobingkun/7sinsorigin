Best Banner to Pull On

Khala and Selective Pick Up are current through the October 8 maintenance. The best choice depends on your account, not on a universal ranking.

Current Short Answer

Choose Khala for the new-hero option, Selective Pick Up for a targeted Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy choice, and save if neither fills a real gap.

Choose Khala If

You want Khala, have reviewed the official details, and can use the standard banner rules: SSR within 80 draws, 50% Pick Up chance, carry-over, and a 120th Draw Bonus.

Choose Selective Pick Up If

You need one of Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy. The other two are excluded and the choice can be changed before the next draw.

Save If

Your roster is stable, your currency is limited, or you want to wait for later official notices. No later successor is confirmed yet.
