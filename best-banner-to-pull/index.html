Best Banner to Pull On

The current banner to compare first is Gowther in Version 1.7. The right answer still depends on what your account is missing, how stable the featured unit is across teams, and whether spending now damages your next-cycle plan.

Current Short Answer

Start with Gowther Pick Up. Pull only if his Lightning, Link, support, or raid-prep routes fix your current Version 1.7 needs; save if he is only the newest SSR on a roster that already functions.

How to Decide

  • Pull for missing account function first: carry, support glue, raid stability, or survival
  • Prefer banners that solve more than one team problem
  • Do not ignore future-banner risk if a better fit is later confirmed

When Saving Is Better

If Gowther only adds a niche upgrade while your account already clears current content, saving is usually the better move until post-maintenance testing proves a stronger team role.

Fast Compare Rules

  • Pick Gowther when his Wand, Grimoire, or Staff route repairs a real account gap
  • Pick saving when your account is stable and his teams do not fit your roster yet
  • Skip for now if the banner does not change progression enough to justify ticket or diamond pressure

What the “best” banner usually means

The best banner is usually the one that changes your account's daily and weekly output, not the one with the highest short-term excitement. That means broader team fit, safer pity use, and fewer wasted upgrade materials.
