Lancelot or Elaine: Should You Pull or Save?

Pull only for a hero you want or a specific roster need, with a spending limit set before drawing. With an unused bonus, staying on one current banner from zero guarantees its featured hero by 120 draws; carried pity can make the required budget smaller.

Rules and content reviewed: October 11, 2026

Which banner fits your goal?

Lancelot’s Heart-Piercing Eyes and Elaine’s Whispers of the Wind run from after the October 8 maintenance until the October 28 maintenance, UTC. Both use Hero Pick Up Draw Tickets and require the main quest HP Recovery! Neither official notice supplies comparative damage tests or a universal tier placement. The recommendations below are account-planning judgments based on the published draw rules.

Record three different values before pulling

SSR pity is the number of draws since your last SSR in Hero Pick Up. An ordinary SSR resets that count, even if it is not the featured hero. Featured guarantee means that after a non-featured SSR, your next SSR is the featured hero of the Hero Pick Up you draw on. The stated 50% is conditional on getting an SSR when you do not have that guarantee; it is not a 50% chance on each draw.

Bonus progress is the total toward the selected banner’s one-time 120th Draw Bonus. Getting the featured hero early does not reset it, and it does not transfer to another banner. Read the selected event’s draw history, guarantee state, and bonus gauge separately. Full hero pools and detailed probabilities are in the in-game Rate Info panel; these notices do not give enough information for an expected-cost or soft-pity model.

How many more draws should you budget?

For the next copy of the selected featured hero, let p be current SSR pity (0–79) and b the selected banner’s bonus progress (0–119), with its bonus unclaimed. If the next SSR is guaranteed featured, the ordinary SSR route needs at most 80 − p more draws. Otherwise, its two-SSR worst-case route is 160 − p. The independent bonus route needs 120 − b. Use the smaller of those two routes as a conservative draw budget, provided you stay on that banner and finish before it closes.

This is a rule-derived upper bound, not an average cost or a promise about when a random SSR will arrive. If the bonus has already been claimed, remove that route and read your current SSR/guarantee state again. The notices do not explain whether receiving the bonus itself changes ordinary SSR pity or the featured-guarantee flag; check the UI after claiming it rather than projecting those states.

Turn a draw budget into a stopping plan

  1. Count usable draws. Include Hero Pick Up tickets already held and currency you can actually convert at the displayed in-game cost. Keep Regular Hero and Exclusive Skin tickets out of this total. Count future event rewards only after confirming you can earn and claim them before closing maintenance.
  2. Choose one objective. “One Lancelot” and “one Elaine” are separate goals. If you have 90 draws with zero pity, no featured guarantee, and zero bonus, you can choose a 90-draw attempt, but cannot call it a guaranteed acquisition.
  3. Set a ceiling you can keep. With 60 pity, a featured guarantee, and 25 tickets, your worst-case target budget is 20, leaving at least five tickets if you stop at the first featured copy. With the same pity but no guarantee and a fresh bonus gauge, 25 tickets cannot ensure that target.
  4. Recheck after every SSR or bonus claim. Update the counters and stop when your objective is achieved. If your first copy arrives at banner draw 20, the unclaimed bonus remains 100 draws away. Decide whether an extra copy merits that new cost before continuing.

Why splitting draws or “building pity” can disappoint

Starting from zero, spending 60 draws on Lancelot and 60 on Elaine leaves each bonus gauge at 60. A possible ordinary-draw sequence is a non-featured SSR at overall draw 80 and no further SSR before draw 120. You have spent 120 draws without obtaining either featured hero or reaching either banner’s bonus. Staying on one banner would have triggered its bonus at 120. This example illustrates a possible outcome, not its probability.

Carryover protects the relevant SSR count and featured-guarantee state, but an unwanted early pickup can use the guarantee on a hero you did not intend to target. Save the tickets instead of drawing just to increase pity on a banner you would regret winning. A nearly full bonus gauge is relevant only if you still want that banner’s reward and can afford the remaining draws; past spending is not a reason to exceed your limit.

Common questions

Does 80 pity guarantee Lancelot or Elaine?

It guarantees an SSR within 80 draws from the last SSR. That SSR is guaranteed to be the featured hero only if the featured guarantee is active; otherwise the notice states a 50% featured chance conditional on the SSR.

Does the 120th Draw Bonus reset after an early pickup?

No. Both current notices say the bonus gauge remains. It is awarded once per banner and cannot be carried to the other banner.

Should I pull because the banner is ending?

Only if your original target and remaining budget still justify it. Check the actual UTC maintenance notice before leaving draws to the last day; the banner notices specify an ending maintenance, not a precise closing hour.
