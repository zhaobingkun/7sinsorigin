Is the Current Banner Worth It?

Khala and Selective Pick Up are current through the October 8 maintenance in UTC. Choose by account need, not by the announcement alone.

Short Answer

Pull Khala if her confirmed role fills a real gap; choose Selective Pick Up if Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy solves a more important account need. Save when neither option improves your next progression goal.

Choose Khala If

You want the new hero, have checked the official details and trial, and accept the standard 80-draw SSR guarantee, 50% Pick Up chance, carry-over rules, and 120th Draw Bonus.

Choose Selective Pick Up If

You need one of Merlin, Elizabeth, or Daisy. The other two are excluded from that draw, and the selected hero can be changed before the next draw.

Save If

Your teams are stable, you lack enough currency for a full plan, or you want to wait for later official notices. No successor after these banners is confirmed yet.
