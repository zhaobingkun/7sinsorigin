Is the Current Banner Worth It?

Ban Pick Up remains current until the September 17 maintenance. Use this page for an account-fit decision, then review the confirmed Khala and Selective Pick Up plans before spending.

Short Answer

Pull Ban only if the hero fixes a real roster or progression gap before September 17. Otherwise, saving for the confirmed Khala or Selective Pick Up window after maintenance is the safer plan.

What Ban Confirms

The official notice gives an SSR within 80 draws, a 50% Pick Up chance, a next-SSR guarantee after a miss, and carry-over for accumulated draws and Pick Up status. The 120th Draw Bonus is once per banner and does not carry over.

Pull If

  • You need Ban's role for a current raid or progression wall.
  • You have tested the trial and understand the resource cost.
  • You can still keep enough currency for your next priority.

Save If

Your teams are stable, Ban duplicates an existing role, or you want to evaluate Khala after the September 17 maintenance. See Upcoming Banners for confirmed timing.
