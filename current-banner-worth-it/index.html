Is the Current Banner Worth It?

Lancelot and Elaine Pick Up are current through the October 28 maintenance in UTC. Choose by account need, not by the announcement alone.

Short Answer

Pull Lancelot if his confirmed kit fills a real gap; choose Elaine if she solves a more important account need. Save when neither option improves your next progression goal.

Choose Lancelot If

You want the new hero, have checked the official details and trial, and accept the standard 80-draw SSR guarantee and 50% Pick Up chance. His banner runs until the October 28 maintenance.

Choose Elaine If

You need Elaine specifically and prefer her confirmed Pick Up window through the October 28 maintenance. Review her official notice and your account plan before spending.

Save If

Your teams are stable, you lack enough currency for a full plan, or you want to wait for later official notices. No successor after the October 28 maintenance is confirmed.
